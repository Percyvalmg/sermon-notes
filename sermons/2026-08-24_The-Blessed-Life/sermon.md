# THE BLESSED LIFE

**Speaker:** Bert Pretorius
**Date:** 2026-08-24
**Source:** 3C Church (3C Live Experience)

---

## INTRODUCTION

Everybody wants to have a blessed life. We often associate a blessed life with a good family, money, open doors, so-called success, influence. But when David speaks in Psalm 32, we see one of the greatest blessings we can experience is not what God gives us, but **what God forgives in us**.

> **Psalm 32:1-2**
> "Blessed is he whose transgression is forgiven, whose sin is covered. Blessed is the man to whom the Lord does not impute iniquity, and in whose spirit there is no deceit."

In this psalm we see the testimony of someone who knows what it feels like to fall and to fail — someone who runs away and hides, like Adam and Eve hid. But it doesn't stop there. This is also someone who fails, hides, and then comes and confesses. Someone who is forgiven. Someone who is restored. Someone who walks away from failure still in the favor of the Lord.

David teaches us that **a blessed life is not a perfect life** — it's a life where you learn what to do when you fail.

The title of this message is **The Blessed Life — from guilt to grace, from hiding to freedom.**

---

## POINT 1 — THE BLESSING OF FORGIVENESS

David uses three words to describe our condition:

- **Transgression** — crossing a line that God has established. You have stepped over. You have transgressed.
- **Sin** — missing God's standard, God's accuracy. The Greek word is *hamartia*, "to miss the mark," like an archer's arrow that misses the target. Sin doesn't mean you're a bad person — it means you're an *inaccurate* person. And as a result of inaccuracy, a dead person.
- **Iniquity** — the crookedness, the deceitfulness, the deviousness of the heart. Doing right, looking good, and nobody else knows — but you know it's for you. *He who trusts his own heart is a fool.*

Sin is not a matter of blanket judgment, black-and-white self-righteousness. It is about accuracy — doing what God wants for your life. What is sin for one person is not necessarily sin for another.

Here is the good news: although the Bible does not minimize sin, it magnifies the grace of God. **Our transgression can be forgiven. Our sin can be covered. Our iniquity can be removed from our account.** That is the gospel of the Lord Jesus Christ.

> **Romans 4:7-8**
> "Blessed are those whose lawless deeds are forgiven, and whose sins are covered; blessed is the man to whom the Lord shall not impute sin."

In Christ, God is not merely improving us. He forgives us. He cleanses us. He justifies us — we are in right standing before Him. And then He restores us. That is the blessed life we receive through His grace.

---

## POINT 2 — THE DANGER OF HIDING

> **Psalm 32:3-4**
> "When I kept silent, my bones grew old through my groaning all the day long. For day and night Your hand was heavy upon me; my vitality was turned into the drought of summer."

Old sin that is not forgiven makes you old. David tried to remain silent. He tried to carry what he should have confessed. What he hid spiritually began affecting him emotionally, and then physically — it affects your mental capacity, and that affects your physical health.

**What we conceal controls us. What is secret controls us. What we cover up controls us.** And the energy it takes to cover, to look better than what you are, wears you out.

There is a difference between covering our own sin and allowing God to cover it. When we cover it, we live in bondage. When God covers it, we live in freedom. We hide because we are afraid of exposure, afraid of what others might say. But God exposes what is destroying us so that He can heal us, so that He can restore us.

*The devil says hide. God says come.*

Judas and Peter both betrayed Jesus. But Judas couldn't believe God would forgive him, couldn't believe the relationship could be restored — so he took his own life. Peter ran to Jesus and received forgiveness and healing.

> **Proverbs 28:13**
> "He who covers his sins will not prosper, but whoever confesses and forsakes them will have mercy."

Even in our courts today, you cannot throw yourself on the mercy of the court until you have admitted guilt. It's the same with God. You have to stop asking *if* — *"Lord, if I sinned..."* — and do the soul-searching. Ask the Holy Spirit, like David did: *Search my heart, O Lord. See if there is any wicked way in me. If there is, expose it. Show me. I don't want to live in "if."*

---

## POINT 3 — THE POWER OF CONFESSION

> **Psalm 32:5**
> "I acknowledged my sin to You, and my iniquity I have not hidden. I said, 'I will confess my transgressions to the Lord,' and You forgave the iniquity of my sin."

We preach the full gospel — not just faith in the finished work of the cross, but also the power of confession. You can't deal with something you don't acknowledge. Dealing with it means acknowledging you have a problem. You don't have to acknowledge it to everybody, but be honest before the Lord: *Lord, this is who I am. Why do I react this way? Why does this person offend me? Deal with my heart. I don't want to carry this burden.*

**Confession is not informing God of something He already knows.** God already knows exactly what you did. Confession is *agreeing* with God about what He already knows.

That is where freedom is — when we stop defending ourselves. Being defensive is such an ugly spirit. God will defend you. God will cover you. God will protect you.

**Freedom starts when we stop blaming others.** Even in the worst circumstances, even if everyone comes against you — if God is with you, you'll be okay. No one has power over you.

**Freedom begins when we stop making excuses.** Even if you didn't get the same opportunities others had, it doesn't matter — God will come through for you.

Freedom is simply saying: *"Lord, I was wrong. Forgive me."* It's not complicated.

> **1 John 1:9**
> "If we confess our sins, He is faithful and just to forgive us our sins and to cleanse us from all unrighteousness."

---

## POINT 4 — GOD BECOMES OUR HIDING PLACE

This is what is so incredible about the gospel: we first see David hiding *from* God. But after his confession, David starts hiding *in* God.

> **Psalm 32:7**
> "You are my hiding place; You shall preserve me from trouble; You shall surround me with songs of deliverance."

So instead of hiding *from* God, you hide *in* God. What a transformation — from hiding in sin to hiding in God. From guilt to grace. From condemnation to deliverance. From silence to singing. The same man who was groaning in verse 3 is suddenly singing in verse 7. That is what grace does.

God does not want us hiding from Him. He wants us hiding in Him. He is our refuge, our security, our protection, our hiding place.

---

## POINT 5 — GOD WANTS TO GUIDE US

> **Psalm 32:8**
> "I will instruct you and teach you in the way you should go; I will guide you with My eye."

Read the whole psalm — don't stop at verse 1. God doesn't only forgive our past. Grace does not stop with forgiveness. Now He wants to guide our future.

> **Romans 6:1-2**
> "What shall we say then? Shall we continue in sin that grace may abound? Certainly not! How shall we who died to sin live any longer in it?"

Why would you want to continue in sin, in death, in inaccuracy? The God who forgives us is also the God who leads us. There are things we do not know — He says, *I will instruct you.* There are things we need to learn — He says, *I will teach you.* There are places we have never been — He says, *I will guide you.* God doesn't just want us to get out of trouble. He wants to teach us how to walk in wisdom.

---

## POINT 6 — DON'T BE STUBBORN

> **Psalm 32:9**
> "Do not be like the horse or like the mule, which have no understanding, which must be harnessed with bit and bridle, else they will not come near you."

Now that you've received forgiveness and grace, receive the instruction too. Receive the template for a victorious life. Some people hit the same wall again and again — sin, hit the wall, come back, "Lord, forgive me, cleanse me, change me." God restores them. They're happy again — and then they turn to the same wall again. And again. God forgives, God instructs, but they don't do what He instructs. He gives wisdom, but they don't do what He says.

**The same grace that forgives you is the same grace that guides you.** You don't have to hit the same wall over and over. But some come up with all kinds of theological reasons why they can keep doing what is toxic to them — even if it isn't sin for someone else, it's sin for them. It destroys them. It destroys their family.

He wants you to become sensitive to His voice. We shouldn't need a crisis before we obey. **Maturity is learning to respond quickly to the voice of God.** When God speaks, obey. When God corrects, repent. When God directs, follow. When God says move, move.

---

## POINT 7 — TRUST CHANGES WHAT SURROUNDS US

> **Psalm 32:10**
> "Many sorrows shall be to the wicked; but he who trusts in the Lord, mercy shall surround him."

Mercy is only for the guilty — only when you confess.

> **Psalm 23:5**
> "Surely goodness and mercy shall follow me all the days of my life."

Unfailing love surrounds those who trust in the Lord. God doesn't promise we will never face difficulty. But He promises that in the middle of everything we face, unfailing love surrounds us.

- When we cannot see the answer, His love surrounds us.
- When we don't understand what God is doing, His love surrounds us.
- When circumstances are changing, His love surrounds us.
- When people disappoint us, His love surrounds us.
- When the future feels uncertain, His love surrounds us.

We may not know what surrounds tomorrow, but **we know who surrounds us today.** We're surrounded by His mercy, His faithfulness, His presence, His goodness, His unfailing love. But that is for those who trust in the Lord.

---

## CONCLUSION & CALL TO ACTION

> **Psalm 32:11**
> "Be glad in the Lord and rejoice, you righteous; and shout for joy, all you upright in heart!"

We are righteous — not because we earned it, but because we are forgiven, covered by the blood of Jesus. We can shout for joy because our hearts are honest before the Lord. Whatever you carry, don't hide it — bring it to Jesus. If you're facing uncertainty, trust God. You might not know where you're going — let God guide you. We are more surrounded by His love than we are surrounded by trouble.

Three decisions for this week:

- **We will not hide.** We will live honestly before the Lord.
- **We will trust.** Even when we don't understand.
- **We will follow.** When He instructs, teaches, and guides us, we will obey.

**Declaration:**

> *Heavenly Father, we declare today, according to Your Word, we are forgiven, cleansed, and restored through the blood of Jesus Christ. We will not live under guilt, shame, or condemnation. We will not hide from our God — we will run to God. The Lord is our hiding place. He preserves us. He delivers us. He instructs us. He teaches us. He guides us. We declare that fear will not surround us. Failure will not surround us. The enemy will not surround us. The unfailing love of God surrounds us. Our families are surrounded by His love. Our children are surrounded by His love. Our homes are surrounded by His love. Our church is surrounded by His love. Our nation is surrounded by His love. Our future is surrounded by His love. We trust You, Lord, and Your love, in the mighty name of Jesus. Amen.*

## SALVATION CALL

Maybe there is somebody here today who has not yet given their life to Jesus. It starts there. Coming to church doesn't make you a Christian. Having a Christian heritage doesn't make you a Christian. Calling yourself a Christian doesn't make you a Christian.

> **John 3:3**
> "Unless a man is born again, he cannot see the kingdom of God."

To be a child of God, you have to be born of God. What must you do? Repent and believe, and you will be saved. Repent means you are done being the god of your own life — turning your back on that, turning toward God, and saying: *"Lord, I trust You. I trust Your word. I surrender myself. Forgive me. Change me. Come dwell within my heart."*

That is when a supernatural miracle takes place. God comes within your heart, takes out the old nature, places His Spirit within you, changes you from the inside. You can modify your own behavior, but you cannot change your own nature. Only God can change you. But for that to happen, you have to receive Him into your heart.

There is people who woke up this morning who will not see the end of this day, and they don't know it yet. If you had to die today, is your life right with God? None of us are guaranteed tomorrow.

**Prayer of salvation:**

> *Dear Jesus, I need You in my life. Please forgive my sin. Take out this old nature. Set me free, Lord, from my past. Everything that I am, I give to You. My whole life I surrender unto You. From today, I follow You and Your word. And thank You, Lord, that according to Your word, I now can say I am a child of God. I belong to You.*
