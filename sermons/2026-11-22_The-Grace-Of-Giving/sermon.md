# THE GRACE OF GIVING

**Speaker:** Bert Pretorius
**Date:** 2026-11-22
**Source:** 3C Church (3C Live Experience)

---

## INTRODUCTION

Every November, without fail, there's one message that has to be preached: **the grace of giving.** Once a year, we deal with the way we manage our finances, and the way we manage our finances has to line up with the purpose of God for our lives.

We're heading into Black Friday. We're heading into December — the month we party, the month we celebrate, the month so many of us spend what we do not have. Just because the ATM says "funds available" doesn't mean it's yours. That's an overdraft. That's a credit card. It still has to be paid.

The last thing you need is to come out of this holiday season needing a 21-day fast just to recover from your sins of spending. And the problem isn't just the 21 days — you have to pay for it for the rest of the year. Yes, you deserve what you have extra. But **pay off your debt and be done with it.**

Our key text today is **Malachi 3:6–12**, and the key word in it is one word: **return.**

## POINT 1 — MANAGE WHAT GOD HAS GIVEN YOU

Before we get to giving, we have to deal with management. Five principles, quickly:

- **Keep good records.** *"Be diligent to know the state of your flocks, and attend to your herds"* (Proverbs 27:23). You have to know what you own, what you owe, what you earn, and where it goes.
- **Plan your spending.** Winging it is not faith. Just giving out is not faith — planning in faith, planning in prayer, is faith. *"The plans of the diligent lead surely to plenty, but those of everyone who is hasty, surely to poverty"* (Proverbs 21:5). Every advertisement is geared toward impulse buying. "Buy now" is a trick from the pit of hell — you don't save money on sale items, you spend it, and the business still profits on the interest.
- **Save for the future.** *"There is a treasure to be desired, and oil in the dwelling of the wise, but a foolish man squanders it"* (Proverbs 21:20). Fools spend whatever they get. Save your 10%.
- **Return 10% back to God.** We'll come back to this.
- **Enjoy what you have.** Don't cry about what you don't have. *"Better is the sight of the eyes than the wandering of desire. This also is vanity and grasping for the wind"* (Ecclesiastes 6:9). If you can't afford a holiday, don't go on holiday. Borrow a tent, pitch it in someone's backyard, and come home grateful you didn't max your credit card.

God's order is simple: **earn it, tithe it, save it, repay debt, enjoy it.**

## POINT 2 — RETURN TO ME, AND I WILL RETURN TO YOU

> **Malachi 3:6–7**
> "For I am the LORD, I do not change; therefore you are not consumed, O sons of Jacob. Yet from the days of your fathers you have gone away from My ordinances and have not kept them. Return to Me, and I will return to you," says the LORD of hosts.

God doesn't give instructions so He can tick a box and grade you good, bad, or "double bad." **God gives instructions and laws to make our lives better.** Holiness means accuracy, purity. Sin — *hamartia* — means missing the mark. If a doctor says take one pill every five hours, you don't get to improvise and take five pills every hour. Accuracy matters.

> **Matthew 7:13–14**
> "Enter by the narrow gate; for wide is the gate and broad is the way that leads to destruction, and there are many who go in by it. Because narrow is the gate and difficult is the way which leads to life, and there are few who find it."

Narrow means accurate. There are not ten ways to get this right — there is one way, and it's God's way. It's difficult because it demands effort, concentration, and precision — like a surgeon who cannot afford to nick the wrong vein. It is not a "by the way" thing — not "oh, I happen to have a five-rand coin in my pocket, I suppose I'll put it in the offering." You have to be accurate in what you give to God, because God is not haphazard.

When a nation moves away from the accuracy of God's word, it means one thing: **they don't believe Him.** If you don't do what God asks, it isn't carelessness — it's unbelief. And that's why the answer is always the same: **return to Me.**

## POINT 3 — WILL A MAN ROB GOD?

> **Malachi 3:8–9**
> "Will a man rob God? Yet you have robbed Me! But you say, 'In what way have we robbed You?' In tithes and offerings. You are cursed with a curse, for you have robbed Me, even this whole nation."

How stupid — to steal from God. The cops might not catch you, but you will be caught. You spite yourself. You hurt yourself. And if you don't conquer the spirit of poverty when you have nothing, you won't conquer it when you have something — the same spirit of lack just follows you through the open door.

Now, understand this: **God is not cursing you.** God does not curse people. He loved you before you ever tithed, before you ever gave your life to Him. But through Adam and Eve, the ground itself was cursed.

> **Genesis 3:17**
> "Cursed is the ground for your sake; in toil you shall eat of it all the days of your life."

The system we operate in — the whole business system, whatever economic model you choose — is a corrupt, cursed system because it runs on cursed ground. You are not cursed because God is punishing you. **You are cursed because what you hold has not been sanctified.**

## POINT 4 — THE TITHE SANCTIFIES THE REST

Here's how it works. Take ten of anything. One of the ten belongs to God — not nine, not eleven, one. Out of a hundred, ten. Out of ten million, one million. **It was never about the amount. It is always about the percentage.**

> **Malachi 3:9–10**
> "Bring all the tithes into the storehouse, that there may be food in My house, and try Me now in this," says the LORD of hosts, "if I will not open for you the windows of heaven and pour out for you such blessing that there will not be room enough to receive it."

God says: "Give Me this one. You steward the other nine. You don't have the right to decide about the one — that's not yours to decide." This is not payment — you were never the owner to begin with. You don't own your breath; you don't pay tax on your breath. You are a steward — of your car, your house, your land, your business, your time. Everything you hold is Lordship, not ownership.

If someone brings a hundred thousand rand out of ten million, he is not generous — **he is a thief**, because he has not been accurate with the percentage. God is not moved by a large number. He is moved by the one out of ten.

So where does the tithe go?

> **Malachi 3:10**
> "Bring all the tithes into the storehouse, that there may be food in My house."

The storehouse is the place where God's purpose is fulfilled — the local church that trains you, equips you, and holds you accountable. Not a television ministry. Not a far-off platform you'll never be known in. **Tithe to your local church** — the place where you are submitted, where you are known, where you are growing.

This is the sequence: as you honor God with what is His first, He sanctifies what remains as yours. If you spend the tithe without first returning it, that spending is cursed — meaning you will make wrong decisions with what's left. Save the tithe first, and what remains, He has a vested interest in protecting and multiplying. **A lot of the Word is not really about the tithe — it's about what happens to the ninety once the ten has gone where it belongs.**

And this is only the tithe — we haven't even reached offering yet. Offering is something else entirely.

> **2 Corinthians 9:10**
> "Now may He who supplies seed to the sower, and bread for food, supply and multiply the seed you have sown and increase the fruits of your righteousness."

God gives you seed to sow and bread to eat — enough to live on, and enough left over to sow and give away.

## POINT 5 — THE PROMISE TO THE OBEDIENT

> **Malachi 3:11–12**
> "And I will rebuke the devourer for your sakes, so that he will not destroy the fruit of your ground, nor shall the vine fail to bear fruit for you in the field," says the LORD of hosts; "and all nations will call you blessed, for you will be a delightful land," says the LORD of hosts.

When you obey, God says He will open the windows of heaven so there is not room enough to contain the blessing, and He will rebuke the devourer on your behalf. He has a vested interest in protecting what you've entrusted to Him — because of your faithfulness in putting His kingdom first.

Being wealthy does not mean you're blessed. Being blessed means fulfilling God's mandate, stewarding what He has given you, and maximizing it for influence — not merely for power. At the end of the day, you can only sleep in one bed, drive one car, use one bathroom at a time, no matter how many you own. The question was never how much you have. It's what you've done with the one.

## CONCLUSION & CALL TO ACTION

Let's stand to our feet. Lift up your hands to the Lord, and let's pray this together:

> *"Lord, today I return to You. Today I return to You. Where I have failed to trust You as my God, please forgive me, please forgive me. Today I make a commitment, today, to be a tither, to be a tither. The spirit of poverty is broken over my life, broken over my life, and over my family and my offspring, my offspring. As from today, my family will be blessed, my family. The generations after me, to the third and the fourth generation, will be blessed because of my commitment to give what belongs to You. Forgive me, Lord, where I've stolen Your money — I've stolen Your money. But I make a commitment, I make a commitment to be faithful, in Jesus' name. Amen and amen."*

## SALVATION CALL

Maybe there's somebody here today who has not yet given their life to Jesus. Unless a man is born again, he cannot enter the kingdom of God — coming to church does not make you a Christian. You have to give your life to Jesus.

Jesus said, *"Today is the day of salvation."* There are people who woke up this morning who will not see the end of this day, and they don't even know it. If you had to die today, is your life right with God? Do not reject the voice of God in your life today. Don't leave this place and risk never sensing your need of Him again — only to stand before Him one day and hear Him say, *"Remember, I spoke to you that day, and you rejected Me."*

If you want to give your life to Jesus, pray this:

> *"Dear Jesus, I need You in my life. Please forgive my sin. Take out this old nature. Fill me with Your Spirit. Everything that I have, I give to You. My whole life, I surrender unto You. And thank You, Lord, that You take control. As I have received You and believe, You give me the right to be called a child of God. And as from now, I belong to You, in Jesus' name."*

Every power of the devil is broken over your life. Every curse is removed. You are in His control now — His hand upon you, His blessing upon you, to become everything He has called you to be, in Jesus' name. Amen and amen.
