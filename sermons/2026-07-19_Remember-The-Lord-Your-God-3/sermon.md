## INTRODUCTION

Last week we talked about families that remember. Our families are to be training grounds, not battlegrounds. For this to happen, we must learn to have honest communication, be willing to have difficult conversations seasoned with grace, and learn to forgive one another.

This week we continue on the subject: **Remember the Lord.**

We are looking at the life of Joseph and drawing lessons from his journey — from the pit to the palace.

---

## POINT 1 — YOUR DREAM IS NOT ABOUT YOU

> **Genesis 37:9-11 (NKJV)**
> Then he dreamed still another dream and told it to his brothers, and said, "Look, I have dreamed another dream. And this time the sun, the moon, and the eleven stars bowed down to me." So he told it to his father and his brothers; and his father rebuked him and said to him, "What is this dream that you have dreamed? Shall your mother and I and your brothers indeed come to bow down to the earth before you?" And his brothers envied him, but his father kept the matter in mind.

God has given each and every one of us a dream, a vision — and that vision is meant to advance **His will** and **His purpose** through and in our lives. But our interpretation of that dream, our perspective, our view about what God is showing us will determine how far we will go in making that dream a reality.

Look at how Joseph perceived the dream. His father rebuked him. Why? Because Joseph's view of the vision was **self-centered and selfish**. It was all about him. In his mind he thought, "I'm going to be the biggest, the greatest, the most powerful — and my brothers, my mom, my dad will bow down before me."

His father, discerning the ways of God, could pick up that this was not the right interpretation. He rebuked him.

**When God gives you a dream, it's not about you. It's about what God wants to do through you and in you.**

The moment you focus on yourself, you are most likely to hinder the very dream God has placed in your heart. The more you become consumed by the fame and what the dream can do for you, you are missing the point. That trajectory will lead you into stealing, cheating, conniving, and manipulating your way into pursuing success.

---

## POINT 2 — DON'T HELP GOD OUT

Remember Abraham and Sarah? When God told Abraham, "You will be a great nation, a mighty nation," and nothing happened in the natural — they thought they could help God out. They came with a plan. A surrogate. And it backfired.

**If you try in your own means and your own efforts to fulfill the dream of God about your life, you will end up going the wrong way.** The dream of God, the vision of God, the plan of God is not about you. It's about God.

---

## POINT 3 — ENVY IS A SELFISH VIEW OF SOMEONE ELSE'S DREAM

The brothers envied Joseph. That's another self-centered, selfish way of looking at vision. You can hear about somebody's greatness and begin to be envious. And envy will lead you into:

- Being disgruntled
- Comparing yourself
- Competition
- Insecurities
- You may even end up murdering a person in your heart

You may wish they don't succeed. Why? Because you wish you were them. They're living your life. **Be careful.**

But a God-centered perspective is what we see in Jacob. He could hear that this boy has got something — there's something God is saying to him — but he's interpreting it the wrong way. He rebuked him, but the Bible says **he kept these things in his heart.** He did not throw away what Joseph said.

---

## POINT 4 — TO REMEMBER IS TO BRING GOD BACK TO CENTER

The word "remember" is two words: the prefix *re* and the suffix *member* — which means to bring something together again. To remember is to bring it back to be a part of something again. It also means to make that thing the major focus of your life. Intentionally focusing on that particular issue.

**To remember the Lord is to intentionally make God a part of your life.**

The assumption is that you have forgotten. You can never be told to remember unless you have forgotten. God has become one of the other options. But to remember the Lord is to bring Him back to His center place, His main place of focus.

---

## POINT 5 — THE PIT: WHERE THE ENEMY CONFINES YOU

> **John 10:10 (KJV)**
> The thief cometh not, but for to steal, and to kill, and to destroy: but I am come that they might have life, and that they might have it more abundantly.

Joseph had to go through a test for the vision of God to ultimately be fulfilled in his life. That test took him through **the pit**, through **Potiphar's house**, through **the prison** — before the palace.

Why? Because God had to drain the self-centered, self-focused, toxic view out of his life. The wilderness is God's training ground to clean you up — all your issues, your self-centeredness, which is likely to hinder the plan of God when it's in full swing.

> **Genesis 37:19-20**
> And when they saw him afar off, his brothers said, "Here is this dreamer. Let's get rid of him."

Don't share your dream with everybody. People may not like it.

The dream — the first thing the devil wants to do is to **steal**. He never comes but firstly to steal. The pit is a place where you are confined. The pit is a place where you have no movement. You are stuck in one place.

**Sometimes the strategy of the enemy is to frustrate the dream of God in your life by placing you in a pit** — a place of limitation, restriction, confinement, no progress. Everybody else is progressing but you are stuck.

How do you come out of the pit? **Only God can take you out.** You can't climb your way out. It's too deep. Only God.

What do you do? You wait on the Lord. How? You **remember the Lord.** You bring Him back to the main issues of your life.

> **Isaiah 40:29-31**
> They that wait upon the Lord shall renew their strength. They shall mount up with wings as eagles. They shall run and not be weary. They shall walk and never faint.

Waiting on the Lord. Looking up to Him. Remembering the Lord and saying, *"God, come again. Come again in my life. Come and rescue me again. I'm in a pit. I am stuck. I thought I would rescue myself. I realize I need You."*

Jonah was stuck in a pit — the belly of the fish — because of his disobedience. Disobedience can take you into a pit.

> **Jonah 2:6-7 (NLT)**
> "When my soul fainted within me, I remembered the Lord and my prayer went up to you, into your holy temple."

How do you come out of the pit? **You remember the Lord through prayer and seeking His face.**

---

## POINT 6 — POTIPHAR'S HOUSE: THE RIGHT FOCUS IN YOUR TRIAL

When Joseph came out of the pit and ended up in Potiphar's house, he did not drown himself in self-pity. He did not say, "They betrayed me. I used to have a family that loved me, but they sold me." He was not stuck in his situation.

He realized: it is what it is. I better do something with the situation. He turned those lemons into lemonades. He remembered the Lord. He brought God back again.

> **Genesis 39:2, 5**
> The Lord was with Joseph, and he was successful in everything that he did. He made him prosper... The Lord blessed the Egyptian's house for Joseph's sake.

**When you remember the Lord despite your situation, the Lord will not just bless you — He will even bless the people around you.** He will bless the people working around you. When you have the right focus in the midst of your trial, the right focus is to remember the Lord.

He could have focused on the betrayal. He could have focused on lost dreams. *I should have. I could have. I would have.* He did not. He said, *"I am here, Lord. I look up to You. Even here, prosper me."* And the hand of the Lord was upon him.

---

## POINT 7 — BEING GOD-FOCUSED PREVENTS SIN

When Potiphar's wife came with her proposal — "Lie with me" — Joseph's response reveals his focus:

> **Genesis 39:9 (NKJV)**
> "How then can I do this great wickedness, and sin against God?"

He doesn't say, "How can I do this against your husband?" or "What will people say?" He's not people-focused. **He's God-focused.** How can I sin and God looks at me? What how do I explain myself before God?

If you remember the Lord and you are God-focused, **you can't sin.** The reason why you fall into sin is because you are thinking nobody sees me. My pastor does not see me. My leader does not see me. My wife doesn't see me. You are looking at people.

**If you can know that God sees you, immediately that will stop you from your tracks.** Because you remember the Lord. He is your focus. People are not your focus. God is your focus.

The sin you do — you're not just harming yourself. You're harming the kingdom. Living wrong, double standards — you are harming God's agenda. That's why Joseph was clear: *How can I sin against God?*

---

## POINT 8 — THE PRISON: KEEP FUNCTIONING IN THE MESS

Joseph ended up in prison because of a false accusation. Once again, he did not drown himself in self-pity. He did not say, "How can God allow this?" Like some of us — when we go through things, the first person we blame is God. *Where are You?*

Joseph did not do that. Joseph said, *"It is what it is. I'm here now. God, show up in the midst of my accusers."*

The more you try to defend yourself, the more you dig a hole. *I'm here now. They say I've done what I've not done. God, what are You going to do for me? Come to my rescue. Remember me, Lord. I am bringing You back. I'm remembering You. I'm making You a part of this mess.*

And in the midst of that, **he continued to do ministry.** He was interpreting dreams. He was talking to people. He was doing stuff.

- You don't stop functioning just because situations are bad
- You don't stop doing ministry just because you are having a bad day
- You don't stop doing ministry just because the chips are down

**When you are in a mess, that's the time when you need to focus on functioning. That's how you get back at the enemy. That's how you fight back. You fight back through ministry. You fight back by continuing to do the will of God.**

---

## POINT 9 — DON'T RELY ON PEOPLE, RELY ON GOD

The cupbearer whom Joseph had prophesied over forgot him for two years. Joseph said, "Remember me." The guy forgot Joseph for two years.

**Don't rely on people. Rely on God.** Don't rely on your contacts and your networks. People will forget you — but God will remember you. Make God part of your life.

When the day came that Pharaoh had a dream — the cupbearer remembered. They brought Joseph before Pharaoh.

> **Genesis 41:15-16**
> Joseph said to Pharaoh, "It is not in me; God will give Pharaoh an answer of peace."

He remembered the Lord even when he was supposed to interpret the dream. He said, **"It's not about me. It's about God."** In the midst of his high profile — he was trending now in Egypt — he did not think about himself. He remembered the Lord.

---

## POINT 10 — THE PALACE: GOD MEANT IT FOR GOOD

After he explained the dream, the Lord remembered him. He became second in charge of everything in Egypt. And the dream came to pass when his brothers stood before him, bowing.

> **Genesis 50:20**
> "You meant evil against me; but God meant it for good."

This self-centered man — after the pit, Potiphar's house, the accusations, the prison — **had changed.** From a self-centered, egotistic agenda to: *God has meant it for good. God sent me on your behalf to go ahead of you and prepare a way for you.*

You thought you were trying to kill me. What you did not know is that you were fulfilling the agenda of God.

---

## CONCLUSION & CALL TO ACTION

Some of you could be going through something right now that doesn't make sense. You love God. You serve God. You've done everything right. But you're going through all kinds of accusations. Everything is falling apart. **Don't curse your wilderness.**

God is forming you into something. When He's done with you, all the toxin of you being self-conscious will be gone. Right now, you are still very self-conscious because you are crying, complaining, whining. It's all about you.

**The longer you complain, the longer the testing.** Why? Because God realizes the self-centeredness is like a stain that needs more time to be erased.

**The quicker you submit, the quicker you bow, the quicker you surrender to the process** — God says, *"Now you are ready. You realize it's not about you. It's all about Me."*

## SALVATION CALL

You are stuck in a pit of life. You can't progress. Things are not happening the way they are supposed to happen. There's accusations around your life. Things are going wrong. You're losing things on the family front, in the finances, in the business, emotionally, health-wise.

**You need Jesus to rescue you out of your pit.** Only Jesus can pull you out. You cannot work yourself out of the pit. Coming to church does not make you a Christian. Following friends who are Christians does not make you a Christian. Having parents who are Christians does not make you a Christian.

You need to make a personal decision: *"Jesus, I need you in my life. Take me out of this pit."*

And for those who love God but have gone through a pit and every experience causes you to disconnect — somehow when life becomes rough, you pull the plug — this morning, the Lord has an appointment with you. He has spoken this word into your life to give you a second chance.

> *Father, in the name of Jesus, I give my life to you. Forgive me for all my sins. Lord Jesus, I surrender to you. You are my Lord. My Savior. My Deliverer. My Restorer.*
>
> *Today I've heard your word. I receive your word. I believe in my heart you have risen from the dead. I confess you are my Lord and my Savior.*
>
> *I thank you for the second chance to remember you and bring you back into my life as the centerpiece. Thank you, Jesus. I will never be the same. In Jesus' name. Amen.*
