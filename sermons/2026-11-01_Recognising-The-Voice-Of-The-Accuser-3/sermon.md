## INTRODUCTION

Over the last few weeks we have been looking at **recognising the voice of the accuser**. What a powerful word. There are so many voices speaking, and we don't always realise what an impact and what an influence these voices have in our lives.

We saw that:

- **Every voice has a source.** Every thought comes from somewhere. It has an origin.
- **Every voice has a character.** It has a personality, a nature. Either the nature of the enemy — to kill, to steal, and to destroy — or the nature of God, to give life and that more abundantly.
- **Every voice has a destination.** The one might look good, but the end thereof leads to destruction. The other might look difficult, but it leads to life.

> **John 10:10**
> "The thief does not come except to steal, and to kill, and to destroy. I have come that they may have life, and that they may have it more abundantly."

The voice of the accuser is the voice that continually **condemns, divides, and captures** people.

And it has cost people dearly. Lives were stolen. Some have sadly taken their own lives, robbing their families of loved ones, because they lent their ears to accusations:

*You're not good enough. You'll never make it in your life. You're a useless father. You're such a failure.*

They listened to those accusations so many days, so many times, that they ended up in a place we never wanted them to end up. Spouses have stepped out of marriages. Companies have split. Communities were broken up and suffered violence — because of accusations.

That is why we need to identify the voice of the accuser — so that we don't fall prey to its character, its nature, and its end result.

Whatever body he comes to you in, if someone is out there accusing anybody in your ears, **it is of the devil.** It can only come from God, or it can only come from the evil one.

Today we take the next step. We have been empowered to identify the voice of the accuser. Now the question is: **How do we cast it down?**

---

## POINT 1 — THE DEVIL ACCUSES, BUT JESUS INTERCEDES

Slander reflects the language and the content of the accuser. **He slanders. He gossips. He accuses.**

Those who make themselves useful to the enemy as accusers and slanderers might be in church. They sing, they bow down, they even shed a tear. But they have the form of godliness and deny the power of it.

> **2 Timothy 3:5**
> "...having a form of godliness but denying its power..."

The power is not in prophecy — the devil can also prophesy. We know there are false prophets. **The power of God is revealed in a life that is changed.**

So you can have the form of godliness. But do you have the power?

- The devil guilts us. **Jesus forgives us.**
- The devil accuses us. **Jesus restores us.**
- While the enemy accuses you, **Jesus intercedes for you.** He stands in the gap.

The devil accuses 24/7, day and night. But we have an Intercessor who never sleeps and never slumbers.

> **Revelation 12:10**
> "...for the accuser of our brethren, who accused them before our God day and night, has been cast down."

> **Hebrews 7:25**
> "Therefore He is also able to save to the uttermost those who come to God through Him, since He always lives to make intercession for them."

As the enemy points to your mistakes, your failures, your weaknesses, Jesus points to His wounds and says, *"Father, I have redeemed this one with My blood."*

And God says, **"Case closed."**

---

## POINT 2 — CONVICTION CORRECTS, CONDEMNATION CRUSHES

**The devil condemns us. The Spirit of God convicts us.**

- Conviction says, *"What you did is wrong. Bring it to God."*
- Condemnation says, *"Hide. Run away. You are worthless. There's no hope for you."*

**Conviction identifies the action. Condemnation attacks your identity.**

You lied. Condemnation says, *"You're a liar. That's who you are."*

- Conviction draws us towards God. Condemnation makes us hide and run away from God.
- Conviction produces repentance. Condemnation paralyses you with shame and hopelessness.
- Conviction says, *"Come back."* Condemnation says, *"Hide. Run. Get out of here."*

God knew when Adam and Eve made their mistake. He was fully aware that they had committed high treason. But in spite of what they did, **He came to pursue them.** That is conviction. *Come back.*

> **Romans 8:1**
> "There is therefore now no condemnation to those who are in Christ Jesus, who do not walk according to the flesh, but according to the Spirit."

**The Holy Spirit convicts to correct us. The accuser condemns to crush us.**

That is why we must learn to discern these voices. It has a source. It has a character. And it surely has a destination.

---

## POINT 3 — AN INNOCENT-LOOKING VOICE CAN TAKE YOU TO A DEVASTATING DESTINATION

Look at David's life.

> **2 Samuel 11:1**
> "It happened in the spring of the year, at the time when kings go out to battle, that David sent Joab and his servants with him, and all Israel... But David remained at Jerusalem."

I don't know what David heard in his mind. I don't know what accusation he woke up with that morning. But it was the time for kings to go to war — and he stayed at home.

He submitted to an accusation:

*No, you're too old now. You're not going to win this one. Remember the fight you had last night with your wife? That was bad. Don't go to war like this.*

It sounds like the voice so many hear on a Sunday morning: *Don't go to church. Stay away. You're not right. You're not good enough to go today.*

David submitted to whatever voice spoke to him, not realising that **it might look innocent, but it has a source.** It came from somewhere. It has a nature. It has a character. And it was taking him somewhere.

So he sent his general to the battlefield and stayed home. Idle. Not fulfilling his purpose — because of a voice that said *you can't, you're not good enough, you're not ready.* He didn't step up into his purpose. **And in idleness, he fell into sin.**

We know what happened. And look at the outcome of what may have started as a small seed sown in the battlefield of the mind:

- Ten of his wives were publicly violated.
- Violence came into his house.
- His own son pursued him and tried to take the kingdom.
- He lost the son born of his sin with Bathsheba — and he lost Absalom.

If David had known that the voice he was following would take him to that destination, **he would never have followed that voice.**

The purpose of this voice is to weaken you in your identity. To humiliate you. To condemn you spiritually. **To kill, to steal, and to destroy from your life.**

So how do we deal with this arrogant voice trying to convince me to end up in disaster? How do I overcome it?

---

## POINT 4 — THE ACCUSER HAS BEEN CAST DOWN

The Word tells us the accuser was **cast down.**

Say it: *Cast down. Cast down.*

> **Luke 10:18**
> "And He said to them, 'I saw Satan fall like lightning from heaven.'"

Thrown out like lightning. **Violently cast down.**

These accusations can come in many forms:

- Accusations I have about myself — self-talk
- Accusations other people have spoken over my life
- Accusations I have about other people's lives
- Accusations in my heart when I stand before God

> **Colossians 2:14–15**
> "...having wiped out the handwriting of requirements that was against us, which was contrary to us. And He has taken it out of the way, having nailed it to the cross. Having disarmed principalities and powers, He made a public spectacle of them, triumphing over them in it."

**Wiped out.** Erased. Stripped. Destroyed. Put out. Blotted out. **There is no evidence anymore.**

There is a way that every argument and every accusation against you can be blotted out. **The letter — the evidence of accusation against you — has been nailed to the cross.**

---

## POINT 5 — JESUS BECAME THE LETTER OF ACCUSATION

We need to understand this: **Jesus Christ became that letter of accusation.**

He was nailed to that cross. He became guilty, sick, broken. He was shamed in front of His loved ones. Cursed. Rejected. All of it on our behalf.

**It was our sins that nailed Him to that cross. It was our accusations that nailed Him to that cross.**

That is why we need a **revelation of the cross** — not a vague understanding, where the cross remains a mystery.

> **Colossians 1:26**
> "...the mystery which has been hidden from ages and from generations, but now has been revealed to His saints."

The truth was there all the time. But to them it was a mystery — until the Holy Spirit revealed it. The reality of the cross, the power of the cross, the substance of the cross can be a mystery to you, while it is a revelation to someone else.

Every specific place on His body where blood was shed, **He dispelled the lies and cancelled the accusations.**

### The Crown of Thorns

The crown of thorns did not happen by accident. Thorns represent the curse.

> **Genesis 3:17–18**
> "Cursed is the ground for your sake... Both thorns and thistles it shall bring forth for you."

The crown of thorns removed every accusation against your financial wellbeing, against your business, against your employment. Whenever an accusation comes telling you why you should not prosper in all things and be in good health, **you can come with the blood that was shed** — and cancel, wipe out, blot out, and put out that fire of accusation burning in your heart.

> **3 John 1:2**
> "Beloved, I pray that you may prosper in all things and be in health, just as your soul prospers."

### His Hands

His perfect hands were nailed to that cross — **to redeem your hands.**

*I can't do it. You'll never be able to do this. It's above you.*

No. His hands were nailed to that cross so that my procrastinating hands, my lazy hands, my unproductive hands **can be redeemed for the glory of God.**

**The cross is the place of exchange for our freedom.**

---

## POINT 6 — IT IS THE TRUTH YOU KNOW THAT SETS YOU FREE

> **John 8:32**
> "And you shall know the truth, and the truth shall make you free."

It is one thing to know *about* the truth. It is another thing to **know the truth.**

Where do you fall short of the truth? Where are you still walking in ignorance? Where does the enemy still have the right to keep you in bondage as a captured slave — not walking in the victory Jesus attained on your behalf?

We all know there is a road that leads to Durban. But knowing there is a road doesn't mean you will reach your destination. **You need to be familiar with the road** — otherwise you will get lost and end up in Cape Town.

It is possible to know that freedom exists, and know nothing about freedom at all.

It is like a bank account lying dormant for years. You are suffering financially, and what you don't know is that an inheritance was deposited there. It rightfully belongs to you. It's yours. But it's a mystery to you.

> **Hosea 4:6**
> "My people are destroyed for lack of knowledge..."

> **2 Peter 1:2–3**
> "Grace and peace be multiplied to you in the knowledge of God and of Jesus our Lord, as His divine power has given to us all things that pertain to life and godliness, through the knowledge of Him..."

Grace — undeserved favour, the result of the cross — is **multiplied to you in the knowledge** of God and of Jesus Christ.

- No knowledge — no grace.
- Little knowledge — little grace.
- Abundant knowledge — the Word of Christ dwelling richly in you — **rich grace.**

If you don't experience it, that doesn't mean it isn't there. **The power is there. The freedom is there.**

---

## POINT 7 — THEY OVERCAME HIM BY THE BLOOD OF THE LAMB

> **Revelation 12:11**
> "And they overcame him by the blood of the Lamb and by the word of their testimony, and they did not love their lives to the death."

This is how they overcame. This is how they wiped out, erased, stripped, destroyed, put out, and blotted out the accuser and the words he spoke — **by the blood of the Lamb.**

Knowing what the blood of Jesus accomplished on your behalf. Knowing the power in the blood. **One drop of the blood of Jesus has the power to restore your marriage**, to deliver your children, to take your children from uselessness to usefulness.

> **Ephesians 1:7**
> "In Him we have redemption through His blood, the forgiveness of sins, according to the riches of His grace."

Every place where Jesus shed blood paid for your redemption.

- **Your hands have been redeemed.**
- **Your feet have been redeemed.** They don't have to take you to wrong places anymore.
- **Your heart has been redeemed.** You don't have to be a slave. You don't have to be a victim of your past anymore.

**The blood of Jesus is ready to speak** on behalf of those who believe.

> **1 John 1:7**
> "But if we walk in the light as He is in the light, we have fellowship with one another, and the blood of Jesus Christ His Son cleanses us from all sin."

Every time you make a mistake and feel condemned, remind yourself: *I am continually being washed.*

*I'm not perfected yet, but I am definitely being perfected. I'm not where I want to be, but I'm not where I used to be. I'm under construction.*

> **Philippians 1:6**
> "...He who has begun a good work in you will complete it until the day of Jesus Christ."

> **Hebrews 10:19**
> "Therefore, brethren, having boldness to enter the Holiest by the blood of Jesus..."

What a joy. When you have fallen, when you have made a mistake, you can come confidently to the throne of grace and say:

*God, I praise You. I come into Your presence — not because I'm so great, not because I'm so sinless, but because You are so awesome. Because the price Jesus paid has set me free.*

**Mysteries belong to Christians. Revelation belongs to disciples.** Don't stay in a place where you are not a disciple.

---

## POINT 8 — THEY OVERCAME HIM BY THE WORD OF THEIR TESTIMONY

We are speaking about a court case here.

- An **accuser** — Satan.
- An **Advocate** — Jesus.
- A **Judge** — our heavenly Father.

What do you do in court? **You testify. You bring evidence.** You have to bring evidence — otherwise you are going to lose the case.

> **Hebrews 11:1**
> "Now faith is the substance of things hoped for, the evidence of things not seen."

*My marriage is broken, God — but I have the substance of what Your Word says. What the blood has done on my behalf. And by the way, Your Honour, I have the evidence of things not seen.*

**We apply the blood of Jesus through our confession and through communion.**

> **Hebrews 4:14–16**
> "Seeing then that we have a great High Priest who has passed through the heavens, Jesus the Son of God, let us hold fast our confession. For we do not have a High Priest who cannot sympathize with our weaknesses, but was in all points tempted as we are, yet without sin. Let us therefore come boldly to the throne of grace, that we may obtain mercy and find grace to help in time of need."

The One who represents me in court, the One who advocates on my behalf, **is sinless — and His sinlessness makes me sinless.**

*He's guilty, Lord. She's guilty. Look what they've done.* But in the throne room of God, they find **mercy.**

*But they are weak. They can't do this. They're useless.* But there is **grace** to empower them to do what they need to do.

**Mercy and grace.**

> **Psalm 107:2**
> "Let the redeemed of the LORD say so..."

Let the redeemed of the Lord say, *"I am redeemed."*

That is why we declare the confessions of the blood every day — in our families, on the road to school, in family devotions, in family prayer.

---

## POINT 9 — THEY DID NOT LOVE THEIR LIVES TO THE DEATH

They did not live to preserve their lives. They were so confident, they had so much faith in the blood, that **they could fight the fight even for other people.**

> **Luke 9:23**
> "If anyone desires to come after Me, let him deny himself, and take up his cross daily, and follow Me."

**Deny what yourself says about you.** Take up your cross and follow Him.

> **Galatians 2:20**
> "I have been crucified with Christ; it is no longer I who live, but Christ lives in me..."

It is no longer I — weak, sinful, useless, pathetic — who live. **It is Christ who lives in me.**

So it is very important that we take these thoughts captive and subject them to what God says about our lives.

> **2 Corinthians 10:5**
> "...casting down arguments and every high thing that exalts itself against the knowledge of God, bringing every thought into captivity to the obedience of Christ."

- **The accuser will forever accuse.**
- **Your Advocate will forever intercede.**
- **The blood of Jesus will forever speak on our behalf.**

- Cast him out of your house.
- Cast him out of your marriage.
- Cancel accusations over your children's lives in the name of Jesus.

**The blood has spoken.**

## SALVATION CALL

You are not here today by accident. God purposed you to be here — no matter what the accuser says.

> **John 6:44**
> "No one can come to Me unless the Father who sent Me draws him..."

You are here today because the Holy Spirit drew you. **He was the voice that nagged in your heart to be here.** And you are here for this very decision.

God wants you to be reconciled to Himself.

> **John 3:3**
> "...unless one is born again, he cannot see the kingdom of God."

How do I become born again?

> **John 1:12**
> "But as many as received Him, to them He gave the right to become children of God, to those who believe in His name."

To sinners. To people who have missed it. To people who struggle. To people who are weak. **To those who receive Him.**

Maybe you received Jesus once, but you are not where you are supposed to be. Today you are coming back to God.

> **2 Corinthians 6:2**
> "...Behold, now is the accepted time; behold, now is the day of salvation."

Not tomorrow. None of us know whether we will have another tomorrow. There is an urgency today. **The Holy Spirit urges you: be reconciled to God.**
