## INTRODUCTION

Remember the Lord your God. Remember. Our text is Deuteronomy chapter 8, verse 2 through verse 18.

**Deuteronomy 8:2–3**
> "You shall remember the Lord your God led you all the way these 40 years in the wilderness, to humble you and test you, to know what was in your heart — whether you would keep His commandments or not. So He humbled you, allowed you to hunger, and fed you with manna which you did not know nor did your fathers know, that He might make you know that man shall not live by bread alone; but man lives by every word that proceeds from the mouth of the Lord."

**Deuteronomy 8:17–18**
> "Then you say in your heart, 'My power and the might of my hand have gained me this wealth.' And you shall remember the Lord your God, for it is He who gives you power to get wealth, that He may establish His covenant which He swore to your fathers, as it is this day."

South Africa, we might be finding ourselves in a time of wilderness. Our economy is not growing the way it should. We're experiencing challenges in all areas of our lives. We've got a youth unemployment which is the highest in the world. Violence in the top three countries in the world. We can say we're finding ourselves in a type of wilderness.

But I've got good news — He says *remember that the Lord your God led you all these 40 years.* And the reason is to humble you, to test you, to know what was in your heart.

Wealth is not just finances. Wealth is in your family, your children, your grandchildren — them fulfilling their mandate and purpose. That's wealth.

---

## POINT 1 — GOD NEVER INTENDED THE WILDERNESS

God never intended Israel to spend 40 years in the wilderness. It was not God's original plan.

God's original plan was to bring Israel into the promised land immediately after the exodus from Egypt. The journey from Mount Sinai to Kadesh Barnea was only a matter of days.

Why did it take them 40 years to get into the promised land?

**40 years was not God's original intention. It was the consequence of Israel's response.** They wandered for 40 years because of unbelief and disobedience.

---

## POINT 2 — THE TURNING POINT WAS THE 12 SPIES

In Numbers 13 and 14, God instructed Moses to send 12 spies into the land. For 40 days, they explored the land. When they returned:

- 10 spies focused on the giants
- 2 spies — Joshua and Caleb — focused on the promise

The 10 said, *"We are not able. Yes, God promised, but we can't do it. It's impossible. It can't be done."*

Joshua and Caleb said, **"The Lord is with us."**

The issue was never the size of the giants. **The issue was the size of their faith.**

Do you believe God or not?

---

## POINT 3 — ISRAEL CHOSE FEAR OVER FAITH

The people believed the negative report. They complained against God. They wanted to return to Egypt. They wanted to go back to slavery. They even talked about appointing another leader.

This wasn't merely fear. **It was rebellion against God's promise.**

Even though they saw the 10 plagues, the Red Sea divided, the manna from heaven, water from a rock — they still refused to trust God.

**Unbelief is not the absence of evidence. It is a refusal to trust God despite His faithfulness.**

**Hebrews 3:18–19**
> "And to whom did He swear that they would not enter His rest, but to those who did not obey? So we see that they could not enter in because of their unbelief."

Not because of the giants. Because of their unbelief.

---

## POINT 4 — MIRACLES DO NOT AUTOMATICALLY PRODUCE FAITH

This is not just an Old Testament story. It gets told through the New Testament.

**1 Corinthians 10:5–7**
> "But with most of them God was not well pleased, for their bodies were scattered in the wilderness. Now these things became our examples, to the intent that we should not lust after evil things as they also lusted. And do not become idolaters as were some of them. As it is written, 'The people sat down to eat and drink, and rose up to play.'"

They sat down. They came out of slavery. They'd been oppressed for 400 years. And now they said, *"I don't want to build. I want stuff for free. I want to sit down. I want to eat, take, consume, drink, and enjoy myself."*

They didn't want to build. They didn't want to fight. They didn't want to stand. They didn't want to enter the promised land.

**Miracles do not produce faith. God's blessing must be met with obedience.**

---

## POINT 5 — AN UNBELIEVING HEART CAN DELAY BUT CANNOT CANCEL GOD'S PROMISE

An unbelieving heart can delay God's purposes even though it cannot ultimately cancel God's promises.

**It can delay God's purpose, but it cannot cancel God's promise.**

Why? Because God will just use somebody else.

If you don't have the faith, God will use someone else. If you don't have the boldness, God will use somebody else. He'll raise up a new generation.

---

## POINT 6 — GOD'S JUDGMENT MATCHED THEIR ACTIONS

**Numbers 14:34** — For every day that they spied, God said, "You'll have a year in the wilderness."

40 years was a judgment on an unbelieving generation. God said, "Everyone 20 years old and above — except Joshua and Caleb and their clan — will die in the wilderness."

**If you don't believe, you will die.**

The generation that continually resisted God will not inherit the land. And that's why God is raising a new generation.

---

## POINT 7 — GOD IS RAISING A NEW GENERATION

We find ourselves in a place where people want to go back to Egypt. A generation that does not believe God. A generation that does not have faith. A generation that cannot declare that Jesus Christ is Lord of all. A generation that has become so humanistic and moved so far from God.

But in the wilderness that we are going through, **God is raising up a new generation.**

A new generation that is humble. A new generation that's dependent on Him.

God wasn't simply taking slaves into a new country. **He was forming a covenant people.** They had left Egypt physically, but now Egypt had to be removed from their hearts.

We have many people that have come out of oppression and slavery, but the slavery is still in them. The oppression is still in them. Why? Because many people just want to sit and eat. Their work is just a job to get money to eat. There's no purpose. Not building the next generation. Not building our children and their children.

Your bodies will be left in the wilderness. You eat, drink, play — and then we bury you. But your children will build the nation. Your children's children will build the nation. Because this is what we're doing — **we're raising up a new generation.**

A new generation that will declare that Jesus is the Lord of our nation. That will not be shy to stand and speak the Word. Stand against corruption. Stand against injustices.

---

## POINT 8 — THE WILDERNESS TEACHES DEPENDENCE AND HUMILITY

Even though the wilderness was not God's original plan — it came because of a lack of faith and dependence and humility — now that we're in the wilderness, God says, **"I still lead you."**

Thank God for His mercy. Thank God for His grace. He still provided in the wilderness to those that have faith, those that would believe.

The purpose of a test is not to expose you or to shame you. God already knows your heart. **God tests you to expose your heart to yourself** — so that you can see where you are.

Humility is difficult to measure when everything's going well. It's only when we are tested that we discover whether we truly believe God.

In the wilderness, there were no farms, businesses, military strength, no income, no visible security. But every day, God provided:

- Food
- Water
- Guidance
- Protection

The test answers questions like:

- Will I trust God when I don't understand?
- Will I obey Him when it's difficult?
- Will I remain faithful and generous when I have a little?
- Will I depend on God or will I depend on myself?

**Pressure does not create the heart. Pressure reveals the heart.**

---

## POINT 9 — GOD'S TESTS PREPARE US TO STEWARD HIS BLESSINGS

God's tests are not designed to make us fail. They're designed to:

- Reveal our hearts
- Deepen our dependence
- Deepen our humility (which is dependence on God)
- Prepare us to steward the blessings God has for us

If you can't handle a thousand, why would God give you two million? If you can't be faithful tithing on a hundred, why would God increase it?

**If you can't share when you have little, when you have much, you're also not going to share.**

---

## POINT 10 — REMEMBER THE LORD WHO GIVES YOU THE ABILITY TO PRODUCE WEALTH

How did you achieve this? *"Oh, it was hard work. Dedication."*

Everybody works hard. The guy who took four taxis to work, gets there at 7:00, leaves at 5, takes four taxis home. Faithful. Looked after his family. So if you say it's the work, then he should have more than you.

No. **It is God.**

Thank you, Lord, for the opportunity. Thank you, Lord, that amidst the lack of my education, I have a job. Whatever the situation is — **it is all God.**

That's why — **remember the Lord.**

Verse 18 says remember the Lord who gives you the ability to produce wealth. And the wealth is there for His purpose. It's for His covenant.

Wealth is not just money. It's position. It's influence. It's the place of privilege.

There's nothing wrong with being privileged. But privilege is the favor of God upon your life — and it's there to empower others. **My privilege is there to empower others.**

- I give my privilege away
- I give my stage away
- I give my intellectual property away
- Everything I am, I sow — that I can raise up the next generation

If you stop sowing, you'll stop reaping. You're setting yourself up for poverty.

This nation is going to be a nation where we can look after nations of the world. God can bless our country to such a degree that we can look after the whole of Africa when needed.

Jesus says, "Now is the time of salvation." Not tomorrow. Now.

> *Dear Jesus, I'm a sinner. I need you, Lord. Therefore today, I give you my life. Everything that I am, I surrender unto you. My whole life I give to you. Thank you, Lord, that you change me, that you forgive me, that you cleanse me. You separate me from my past. And as from today, I'll never be the same again. I belong to you. I am yours.
