## INTRODUCTION

Some souls are struggling to discern the voice of God. And so the word is prepared — because when the Bible speaks about a thing, it doesn't hand you one little verse.

People are being swayed. Swayed by voices that are not God's voices, but clothed in spirituality. **The devil does not show up and shout, "I'm the devil."** He comes clothed as a lamb.

The title this morning is **recognising the voice of the accuser**.

The text is Revelation 12:10 — the verse *before* the one everybody loves.

> **Revelation 12:11**
> "And they overcame him by the blood of the Lamb, and by the word of their testimony; and they loved not their lives unto the death."

You love verse 11. Through the power of the blood they were not afraid to die. There was courage. But **you cannot overcome the enemy if you don't know the enemy.** So look at verse 10:

> **Revelation 12:10**
> "...for the accuser of the brethren is cast down, which accused them before our God day and night."

That includes the sistren too.

**He has been cast down. He has been cast down. He has been cast down.**

Every voice has a source. Every voice has a character. And every voice has a destination. There is a voice that has a character and it is taking you somewhere.

So the question is not only *is what I'm hearing true?* The question is: **where is this voice taking me?**

- Does it lead me toward repentance and restoration — or toward shame, bitterness, accusation, and division?
- Does it make me want to pray for people, or does it make me want to talk about people?
- Does it produce forgiveness, or does it feed offense?
- Does it sound like the accuser, or does it sound like the restorer?

The voice of the accuser condemns. It divides. It captures and oppresses. The voice of Jesus convicts — but it restores and sets free.

*Which voice are you listening to? Which voice are you becoming?*

We've been looking at the satisfied life. We looked at the importance to forgive, because forgiveness releases us from bitterness, releases us from debt, and releases revenge into the hands of God. Today we go a little deeper. Because behind accusation, behind condemnation, behind slander and division, there is a spiritual force at work.

> **Ephesians 6:12**
> "For we wrestle not against flesh and blood, but against principalities, against powers, against the rulers of the darkness of this world, against spiritual wickedness in high places."

If you are slandering people, putting people down, you have no understanding of the spiritual world. None at all. You are playing with forces that will destroy you and destroy your family.

Behind condemnation, slander and division there is a spiritual force. Behind forgiveness, mercy, intercession and restoration there is an opposite force. One is the heart of the devil. One is the heart of God. **Are you able to discern the difference?**

Being right is overrated — because it's *your* righteousness, *your* context, what *you* think. Sometimes people who are factually right are spiritually wrong. The issue may be true. The failure may be real. The sin may have happened. But the question is: **what is your purpose when you expose it?**

- Is it to restore, or to destroy?
- Is it to heal, or to humiliate?
- Is it to reconcile, or to divide?

Or is it to intercede — like Jesus, seated at the right hand of the Father, making intercession for us? Every time the accuser comes and says, *"This is what Bert did,"* Jesus stands up, holes in His hands, and says, *"Father, his sins are forgiven."*

He intercedes on our behalf.

## POINT 1 — THE DEVIL IS CALLED THE ACCUSER

The Greek word for devil is *diabolos*. **Diabolos means slanderer.**

So when you say "devil," you are saying "slanderer." Where the Bible names the devil and where it speaks of a slanderer, it uses the same word — *diabolos*. And he accuses day and night.

The devil accuses on four fronts:

1. **He accuses us before God.** That is his main work.
2. **He accuses others to us.** You're minding your own business, watching the football game, and suddenly a thought comes up: *"Remember what that guy said to you yesterday?"* Your team is losing and now your mind is going. *"I let it go yesterday, but now that I think about it again — what did he really mean?"* That's the ministry of accusation.
3. **He accuses us to ourselves** until we feel condemned: *"Look what you did. You rubbish. You did it again. This is who you are. You can't help yourself. You're not a child of God. You're faithless."*
4. **He gets us accusing one another.**

That is the force, the main work and ministry of the devil. And he brings division.

- He divides marriages.
- He divides families.
- He divides churches.
- He divides leaders.
- He divides the workplace.
- And ultimately, he divides the nation.

Listen to how politicians speak. *"Don't trust that one. Remember the past. That's their nature — they don't change."* The devil divides the nation, and politicians play that game to get power. If you are insecure and not secure in God, it plays to your ears.

*"I've got to be among my people."* My people — do you mean Christians, or do you mean a tribe? My people — do you mean children of God, or a skin colour? Half of us are so mixed we don't even know where we come from.

## POINT 2 — SLANDER IS THE LANGUAGE OF THE ACCUSER

> **2 Timothy 3:2-4**
> "For men shall be lovers of their own selves, covetous, boasters, proud, blasphemers, disobedient to parents, unthankful, unholy, without natural affection, trucebreakers, false accusers, incontinent, fierce, despisers of those that are good, traitors, heady, highminded, lovers of pleasures more than lovers of God."

They will be unloving, unforgiving, and slanderers. Without self-control — you want what you want, you don't get it, and now you're out of control, using force, using violence to get your way. *"I have the right. I deserve."*

Traitors. You slander your own friend because you want the job. You've worked with a man for five years, the promotion comes up, and you throw him under the bus very quickly. That is the same spirit.

And that word "false accusers" — *diaboloi*. It means devil.

Now we are **not** calling people devils. But understand this: **when we slander, accuse, and deliberately destroy another person's reputation, we are participating in a strategy that does not reflect the heart of Christ.**

When unforgiveness enters the heart, accusation comes from the mouth.

## POINT 3 — THE DEVIL ACCUSES, BUT JESUS RESTORES

Look at the two. The spirit of the devil — his very name is accuser, slanderer. Now look at the heart of Jesus.

Look at the woman caught in adultery. They bring her to Jesus. **She was guilty.** Jesus was not dealing with a false accusation. It was a fact. She had sinned. And the religious leaders said, *"Moses said she should be stoned. Stone this woman!"*

Everybody has a stone. Everyone has an accusation. Everyone knows what she did. Everyone is ready to condemn her.

> **John 8:7**
> "...He that is without sin among you, let him first cast a stone at her."

And all the stones fell to the ground.

> **John 8:11**
> "...Neither do I condemn thee: go, and sin no more."

Understand the heart of Jesus. He does **not** deny the sin. He does **not** celebrate the sin. He does **not** redefine the sin. He says, "Go and sin no more" — that is the truth. But before He says it, He says, "Neither do I condemn you" — that is grace. Jesus is full of grace *and* full of truth. There must be a balance between truth and grace.

- The accuser only wanted to speak about what she had done. **Jesus was interested in what she could become.**
- The accuser saw her past. **Jesus saw her future.**
- The accuser wanted stones. **Jesus wanted restoration.**
- The devil wants your failure to become your identity. **Jesus wants His grace to become your identity.**

It's simple. Do you have the voice of a restorer, or the voice of an accuser? What are you listening to?

## POINT 4 — A FORM OF GODLINESS WITHOUT POWER

> **2 Timothy 3:5**
> "Having a form of godliness, but denying the power thereof: from such turn away."

Run. Run. Run. Turn away.

Paul describes people who are slandering, unforgiving, proud, unloving, self-centred — *and yet* they still have a form of godliness. They look spiritual. They speak the Christian language. *"God is good all the time."* They say amen and hallelujah at the right time. They can prophesy, *"So sayeth the Lord."* They quote the Bible, go to church, pray publicly, carry a title.

But something is missing. **Power.** The power that changes you on the inside.

You sing, *"I love You, Lord,"* you leave that door, somebody drives in front of you before you've even left the property — and what comes out of your mouth? Someone bumps you by accident in the crowd — what's in your spirit?

What power? The power to forgive. The power to deal with offense. How we respond when someone else fails, when someone disappoints us.

**True godliness is not just about what we profess. True godliness is what Christ produces in us.**

> **Titus 1:16**
> "They profess that they know God; but in works they deny him, being abominable, and disobedient, and unto every good work reprobate."

They profess Christ with their lips but deny Him in the way they treat people. We sing about grace but refuse to show grace. We preach forgiveness but live in bitterness. We talk about love while continually slandering someone — the ministry of the devil.

- If my Christianity gives me enough religion to judge everybody else but not enough grace to forgive, something is wrong.
- If I can worship God on a Sunday but destroy somebody's reputation on a Monday, something is wrong.
- If I can speak spiritual language but continually slander, accuse and divide, I have a form of godliness without the power.

> **Matthew 23:27**
> "...for ye are like unto whited sepulchres, which indeed appear beautiful outward, but are within full of dead men's bones, and of all uncleanness."

You look beautiful on the outside, but as soon as you open your mouth the stench of death comes out.

So the issue is not *do I look spiritual?* The question is: **has the power of God changed my heart?**

Because anybody can hold a grudge. You don't need the Holy Spirit to be bitter. You don't need spiritual maturity to gossip. You don't need the power of God to take revenge. The flesh does that all by itself.

- But to forgive someone who hurt you — that takes grace.
- To pray for someone who betrayed you — that takes grace.
- To refuse to slander someone when you have information that could destroy them — that takes character.
- To seek restoration rather than humiliation — that is the power of God working within.

Forgiveness is not weakness. Slander is a weak person. A gossiper is a weak person — taking hands with the devil, going to bed with the devil, because you can't sleep at night, because you're bitter, because you haven't taken it to God, because you want control and you have no self-control.

Be careful of spiritual language without a Christ-like heart. Some of the strongest accusations are wrapped in religious language: *"I'm just telling the truth. I'm exposing darkness. I'm standing for righteousness. People just need to know."* Well — why don't we take *your* life and let people know all *your* stuff?

Examine your heart. Is it truly about righteousness, or are you just offended? Is it about protecting people, or do you enjoy exposing people? Is it correction, or revenge? Not everything said in the name of truth carries the heart of Christ.

> **Ephesians 4:15**
> "But speaking the truth in love, may grow up into him in all things, which is the head, even Christ."

**Truth without love becomes a weapon.** Grace and truth go together. Not truth without grace. Not grace without truth.

## POINT 5 — TURN AWAY AND GUARD YOUR HEART

And the Bible says, from such people turn away.

Forgiveness does not mean unlimited access. Sometimes the situation requires distance. Grace does not mean you continually participate in gossip. If someone continually slanders, continually accuses, continually divides, continually feeds offense and refuses correction, Paul says: **do not let that spirit shape you.**

There are conversations you need to walk away from. And this is especially for young people — it affects your direction. It affects whether God's grace and favour are on your life.

> **Matthew 5:7**
> "Blessed are the merciful: for they shall obtain mercy."

If you're not merciful, there's no mercy for you. When conversations like that start, you get up and you walk away.

Things to leave behind:

- Gossip circles you need to leave.
- WhatsApp groups you need to stop feeding — the compound groups, the homeowners groups, the school board groups.
- Fights God never intended you to pick.
- People you may love, but who must not be allowed to disciple your heart.

> **1 Corinthians 15:33**
> "Be not deceived: evil communications corrupt good manners."

What's a good habit? Forgiving. Restoring. Evil company corrupts it.

This does not mean we never deal with issues — that's another sermon, taught the godly way in the weeks to come. But you cannot be a slanderer.

- You can forgive someone and still create distance.
- You can love someone and still refuse to participate in the slander.
- You can praise someone and still say, *"I will not allow the spirit of accusation to enter my heart."*

What's the evidence? A changed life. Ask yourself: what voice am I allowing to shape me? After someone has spoken to you, if you become more bitter, more suspicious, more cynical, more unforgiving, more dividing — you need to ask what voice is shaping your future and your destination.

He has given us the power of a transformed life:

- The power to forgive.
- The power to love.
- The power to restore.
- The power to speak life.
- The power to walk away from an accusation.
- The power to become more like Jesus.

Don't settle for the form. Live in the power of God.

Our conduct can contradict our confession. And the clearest evidence that the gospel has power is not the ability to identify someone else's sin — it's that Christ is working with us, in us, and changing us.

## ILLUSTRATION — LEARN TO DUCK, AND LET JESUS RESTORE

Jesus is with His disciples in the Garden of Gethsemane. They come to arrest Him. Peter — not quite saved yet, though he's walked with Jesus for three years — pulls out a sword in church. He wasn't aiming for an ear. He tried to kill the man. The man ducked, and lost his ear instead.

So the first lesson in church: **learn to duck.** We've got all sorts in church, so sometimes you need to duck.

But watch what Jesus does. The ear is on the ground, the man is hurt — and Jesus goes to the hurt person, takes the ear, and restores the wound. And with Peter, He doesn't ban him. He rebukes him, chastises him, corrects him harshly — but He doesn't write him off.

Some of your ears have been cut off. You've been in cell and you didn't duck in time. Because people have issues — all of us have bad days. But where you have been hurt, God will heal you and restore you. And if you were the one who did the hurting, God will forgive you and change you — just as Peter became one of the greatest apostles of all time.

So deal with your stuff. But ask the real question: **do you have the spirit of the accuser, or the spirit of Christ? Is it restoration, or is it slander?**

## A WORD ON LEAVING IN OFFENSE

When people leave a church caught in that spirit, here's the problem: your spirit leaves with you. Leaving the church doesn't change anything. If you take offense and leave, the accuser goes with you — still in your marriage, still in your home, still with your children, still in your workplace. You're not a restorer.

And if you manipulate others to take offense on your behalf — twenty, thirty people — you will stand before God and answer for every soul you took with you.

This is not manipulation or control. There's no demand that you stay forever. But don't be the accuser. Don't be the slanderer. Deal with your stuff, and let God make you a restorer.

## CONCLUSION & CALL TO ACTION

Are you captured by the slanderer? Are you captured by the accuser — in your workplace, at your school, in your cell, in your marriage, in your relationship with your kids and grandkids?

Have you allowed offense to define your relationship with grandkids who aren't visiting, or children you haven't seen in three years? Don't take offense. Don't drive them further away. Don't become an accuser. Don't become a slanderer.

People who wronged you, who stole your promotion — don't be a slanderer, don't be a gossip. We forgive. God will give you a better promotion. He'll give you a better breakthrough.

Pray this together:

> *Heavenly Father, please forgive me. We have taken hands with the accuser. We have taken hands with the slanderer. Please forgive me. I make a decision today to trust You. I don't want a form of godliness — I want the power of God within my heart, having a transformed life. Thank You, Lord, that You help me to forgive, because out of myself I can't do this. Help me, Lord, to forgive this offense, and rip out this root of bitterness and change my heart. Forgive me, Lord, for being offended, for being bitter, for being an accuser, for being a slanderer. But today I decide to be a restorer — one that forgives, a builder — because that's Your heart, and You dwell within me. You give me the ability to do it. In Jesus' name, amen and amen.*

## SALVATION CALL

Every head bowed, every eye closed.

If you have not yet given your life to Jesus, now is the time. You can't live like this without God in your life. Coming to church doesn't make you a Christian. Calling yourself a Christian doesn't make you a Christian. **You must be born again.**

To be a child of God, you have to be born of God. It means you repent and say, *"Lord, I'm done with my old life. I'm finished being my own god, full of my opinion, thinking I'm right and self-righteous. No more. I turn to You, Lord. Be my God. Forgive me. Change me. Help me."*

When you invite God into your life, He will come and change you. He'll change your nature, and you'll never be the same again.

The Bible says now is the time of salvation — not tomorrow. He says *now*. There are people who woke up this morning who will not see the end of this day. If you had to die today, is your life right with God?

Pray this prayer:

> *Dear Jesus, I need You in my life. Please forgive my sin. Take out this old nature. Fill me with Your Spirit. Thank You, Lord — You changed me today. Everything that I am, I give to You. And thank You, Lord, that from now my life belongs to You. I am Yours, in Jesus' name.*

And Lord, I thank You that every power of the devil is broken over their lives, every curse removed. Draw a hedge of protection around them. Lead and guide them in everything they are and do, in Jesus' name. Amen and amen.

**You now belong to Jesus. Hallelujah.**
