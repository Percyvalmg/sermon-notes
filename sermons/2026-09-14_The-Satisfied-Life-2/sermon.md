## INTRODUCTION

We continue with part two of the satisfied life. **The satisfied life. Learning the secret of contentment.**

And the bottom line is this. **Christ is enough.** Say it with me. Christ is enough.

> **Philippians 4:11**
> "Not that I speak in regard to need, for I have learned in whatever state I am, to be content."

Note those words. **I have learned.**

> **Philippians 4:12-13**
> "I know how to be abased, and I know how to abound. Everywhere and in all things I have learned both to be full and to be hungry, both to abound and to suffer need. I can do all things through Christ who strengthens me."

We use verse 13 a lot. Just before you write those exams. They tattoo it on their body, they put it on their shoes. *I can do all things through Christ.* No. That verse is taken out of context. Paul is not saying Christ will give you the strength to get everything you want. **He's saying Christ gives you the strength even when you don't have what you want.** That changes the meaning.

And the New Living Translation doesn't just say *I have learned.* It says *I have learned the secret.* Which means, first of all, that contentment is a process of development. It is not instant. It's something you apply, something you work at, something you consciously decide in faith to apply within your life.

---

## POINT 5 — CHRIST IS ENOUGH MEANS MORE THAN ACHIEVEMENT

We are Pentecostal charismatics, so we always want instant. We want what we want now. We want a miracle. *It must happen now.* We pray over it, we prophesy over it. No. There's process.

When a child is born, you can't pray, *"I prophesy over you, you'll be 18 in two years."* No. You've got to feed the thing three times a day. Protect it, look after it, clean it. And sometimes it's not 18 — it's until they're 40.

So contentment is not *we pray over you, we prophesy over you, we drive out the demon of discontentment,* and suddenly you're content. It doesn't work that way. It's a process of learning to trust God. My circumstances change, but my Christ does not change.

> **Hebrews 13:8**
> "Jesus Christ is the same yesterday, today, and forever."

That's why verse 13 says *I can do all things through Christ.* Not through achievement. Through Christ.

- I can have much and remain humble.
- I can have little and be grateful.
- I can be celebrated and be grounded.
- I can be rejected and still remain secure.
- I can walk through an open door, and I can survive a closed door.
- I can enjoy abundance without being controlled by it, and endure lack without being destroyed by it.
- I can lose something and still worship God, and gain something and still remain surrendered to God.

Because my strength isn't coming from what I have. **My strength comes from the Lord Jesus Christ.**

---

## POINT 6 — GOD WANTS TO BREAK THE POWER OF MORE

Our culture keeps telling us more. More money. More fame. More recognition. More followers. More possessions. More success. More influence. More comfort.

But we want more, and we don't even maximize what we already have. You want more, but you're not using what you do have to the fullest.

Jesus says something completely different.

> **Luke 12:15**
> "Take heed and beware of covetousness, for one's life does not consist in the abundance of the things he possesses."

That's revolutionary. He's saying your life is bigger than what you own. **Your life is not your bank balance.** Your identity is not your position or your skill. Your significance is not in your title.

Nothing wrong with titles. Nothing wrong with showing respect. But when a title becomes your identity, your value is now determined by how many people know your name. In actual fact, you *devalue* yourself when you compare yourself to anything in the physical — your skin color, your tribe, your language. If that is your identity, you've valued yourself far below what God thinks of you. That's why you're insecure. That's why you have to fight for your existence, and you get sensitive, and that button gets pushed, and you have a little hissy fit.

But when you know your identity is in Christ, those things don't move you. **Your life is bigger than what you own.**

---

## POINT 7 — WHEN CHRIST BECOMES YOUR TREASURE, EVERYTHING ELSE LOSES ITS GRIP

> **Philippians 3:7-8**
> "But what things were gain to me, these I have counted loss for Christ. Yet indeed I also count all things loss for the excellence of the knowledge of Christ Jesus my Lord... and count them as rubbish, that I may gain Christ."

Yes, the Bible uses the word rubbish. I'll say it again — the Bible uses the word rubbish. Your car, rubbish. Your house, rubbish. Your position, rubbish. Your influence, rubbish. In comparison to Christ. If you know what God has done for you.

This is about eternal value. That's why we need vision. People suffer and struggle because of a lack of vision. You don't see. You're caught down in the physical, your eyes are down, you're on yourself. And if you're looking at yourself the whole time, you can't see, you have no foresight. Yet you're making decisions every day — not just for you, but for your family, your kids, your grandkids. You've got to lift up your eyes off yourself, off your issues, off your life. And the only way you do that is when you realize **Christ is enough.**

That doesn't mean your possessions are evil. It just means they don't possess you.

- Your possessions must not possess you.
- Education is important, but it doesn't possess you.
- Your position is important to bring order and structure, but it does not possess you. It's a means to an end. It's not your identity.

So we can have things without things having us. We can enjoy God's blessing without worshiping it. We can carry influence without needing applause. We can succeed without success becoming our identity. Why? Because we possess the greatest treasure, and that is Jesus Christ.

> **Matthew 6:21**
> "For where your treasure is, there your heart will be also."

So the question isn't *what do we have?* The question is *what has your heart?* Where is your heart?

> **Matthew 13:44-46**
> "The kingdom of heaven is like treasure hidden in a field, which a man found and hid; and for joy over it he goes and sells all that he has and buys that field. Again, the kingdom of heaven is like a merchant seeking beautiful pearls, who, when he had found one pearl of great price, went and sold all that he had and bought it."

When you discover the treasure, you do whatever it takes to get hold of it. And when you find something that valuable, **surrender stops feeling like loss.** *I must give up my time, my Sunday, my Monday.* No. When you've found something of greater value, surrender is no longer loss.

> **Philippians 1:21**
> "For to me, to live is Christ, and to die is gain."

Even if I die, I'm with the Lord. So I'm not scared to die.

> **Revelation 12:11**
> "And they overcame him by the blood of the Lamb and by the word of their testimony, and they did not love their lives to the death."

The New Living Translation says *they were not afraid to die.* And if you're afraid to die, you'll be afraid to live.

---

## POINT 8 — A BELIEVER WHO KNOWS CHRIST IS ENOUGH CANNOT BE CONTROLLED

So how do you threaten somebody who believes this?

- Take my possessions — Christ is enough.
- Take my stuff — Christ is enough.
- Take my job — Christ is enough.
- Reject me — Christ is enough.
- Walk me through difficulty — Christ is enough.
- Bless me — and Christ is still enough.

That kind of believer is very difficult to control. I'm not talking about people who call themselves Christian but aren't. I'm talking about people who have experienced the forgiveness and the grace of God, whose lives are changed and transformed, who don't live for themselves but live for others. **You cannot capture that person.**

The church has a voice. Millions of people are in church every Sunday, and their pastors are ministering to them. That's a voice. People get worked up thinking the media is power, that social media is power, that followers are influence. What are followers? What are views? A pastor came to me very worked up because an influencer said something about me and it had two hundred thousand views. I said, *"Two hundred thousand? Is that all? When they attack me it's usually two, three, four million."* But it has no influence. It doesn't mean anything.

You cannot control someone who is surrendered.

So bump your neighbor and ask: **Are you captured, or are you surrendered?** If you're not surrendered, you're captured — in other words, you can be bought. What's your price? Your price is your thing. Your little thing. That's why we need God in our life every single day. We can be right for a year, two years, three years, and just one day go wrong.

> **Proverbs 28:26**
> "He who trusts in his own heart is a fool, but whoever walks wisely will be delivered."

You've got to check your heart every day. You start off doing it for the right reason, and then God blesses you, and money starts coming, and before long you need that money to sustain it — and now you're driven by the money instead of the heart. It doesn't take long to flip you.

So come before the Lord and ask: *Lord, am I captured? Am I doing this for the money?* You don't work just for money. You're not a slave. God placed you there for a reason. Yes, it provides — but there are lives you need to touch there. There's a young man, a young woman who needs your guidance, your experience, your help to get through. Get your purpose beyond the paycheck. Fulfill the mandate. But you'll only think like that when Christ is enough.

**That's why he says, I have learned. Say it with me. I have learned.**

## CONCLUSION & DECLARATION

*(In closing, Pastor Bert testified how God taught him this secret through a season of pressure over his home — a dispute that threatened to cost him everything he owned. In the middle of it he developed shingles from stress, and his pastor taught him to do devotion daily through the confessions and the sheddings of the blood of Jesus.)*

**The five confessions of the blood:**

> *By the blood of Jesus, I am redeemed from the power of the enemy. By the blood of Jesus, I am forgiven of all my sin. By the blood of Jesus, I am cleansed now and continually from all my sin. By the blood of Jesus, I am justified — God sees me as though I've never sinned. By the blood of Jesus, I am sanctified, set apart for purpose.*

Over fourteen, fifteen, sixteen months of walking this out — not one Sunday, not one touch of the Holy Spirit, but a process — I learned to live one day at a time. And I got to the place where I genuinely didn't care about the property. I didn't care that I could lose millions, lose everything. I was prepared to release it, because I know I'm saved, and God will still help me. And when the final meeting came, God moved in a way I could never have arranged. But God had to teach me first. **I had to learn the process that Christ is enough. If I have to die today, I'm fine.**

There are things God wants to give you that He can't give you yet, because they would kill you. That million might take you to hell. That promotion might destroy you. So He teaches you the secret first.

God has so much He wants to do in and through you. He wants to take you to places. You've got nations on the inside of you. Lives are going to be touched. Transformation is going to take place. But you've got to learn the secret that Christ is enough.

Learning the secret of contentment means taking what you have and using it.

> **Acts 3:6**
> "Silver and gold I do not have, but what I do have I give you: In the name of Jesus Christ of Nazareth, rise up and walk."

*"I've got nothing to give"* is a lie out of the pit of hell. You've got so much in you. You've got so much to give. People are crying and dying — they just want your love, a hug, a smile, your attention, a little guidance. This country needs you. But right now, if you're captured, you're useless — just getting by, just looking after yourself until you die. No. You are not useless. There is wealth on the inside of you.

**Declaration:**

> *Heavenly Father, we receive Your word. We declare that Christ is enough for us. Christ is enough. We will not be controlled by possessions, by positions, by recognition, or by success. We will remain faithful in abundance and faithful when we're in need. We will carry success without pride. We will walk through difficulty without losing faith. Our identity is in Christ. Our security is in Christ. Our future is in Christ. Whatever comes, whatever goes, Jesus remains. And through Christ who strengthens us, we can face every season and remain faithful. We submit to the process of learning the secret that Christ is enough. Lord, help us to learn, help us to grow, in Jesus' name. Amen.*

**Say it: Christ is enough.**

## SALVATION CALL

Every head bowed, every eye closed. If there's somebody here who has not yet given their life to Jesus — calling yourself a Christian doesn't make you a Christian. Going to church doesn't make you a Christian.

> **John 3:3**
> "Unless a man is born again, he cannot see the kingdom of God."

You can't be a child of God if you're not born of God. So how do you get to that place? You come to the Lord and say, *"Lord, I'm done serving myself and living life according to my own opinion. I need You — as a wife, as a husband, as a parent, as a citizen of this nation. I need You, Lord. Forgive my sin."* God will take out that old nature and place His Spirit within you. It's a supernatural miracle. It's not a feeling, not an emotion — it's a decision. And the Bible says He gives you the right to be called a child of God.

You can modify your behavior for a while through discipline, but you can't change your nature. You can't change your heart. You are who you are — unless you come to God and let Him do it within your life.

Jesus said, *"Today is the day of salvation."* Not tomorrow. Today. No one is guaranteed tomorrow. People woke up this morning who will not see the end of this day. You'll stand before God one day. Don't let it be the day you remember rejecting Him.

**Prayer of salvation:**

> *Dear Jesus, I need You in my life. I am a sinner. I acknowledge that I need You. Please forgive my sin. Take out this old nature. Fill me with Your Spirit. Everything that I am, I give to You. My whole life I surrender unto You. I receive You into my life. I trust You, and I trust Your Word — that if I receive You and believe, I have the right to be called a child of God. Thank You, Lord, that from now I belong to You, and nothing can snatch me out of Your hand, because I am Yours. In Jesus' name. Amen.*

Every power of the devil is broken over these lives, every curse removed. The Lord draws a hedge of protection around you and leads you in all things. We belong to Jesus. **Christ is enough.**
