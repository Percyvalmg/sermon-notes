## INTRODUCTION

We have been walking through this series on the satisfied life. Pastor Bert and Pastor Chené have laid a powerful foundation. This morning we return to the text we have been living in, Philippians 4.

**Philippians 4:11-13**
> "Not that I speak in regard to need, for I have learned in whatever state I am to be content. I know how to be abased, and I know how to abound. Everywhere and in all things I have learned both to be full and to be hungry, both to abound and to suffer need. I can do all things through Christ who strengthens me."

We love to read that last verse through the lens of our needs, our ambitions, our desires. *"I can do all things."* But Scripture is not ultimately about what we can accomplish.

When Paul wrote this, the emphasis was never that *he* could do all things. The subject of the verse is **Christ**. Sometimes we read Scripture and make ourselves or our circumstances the subject. But Christ is always the subject of Scripture.

The important part of this verse is not *I can do all things*. The important part is that everything is **through Christ**.

Paul was not saying, "Because Christ is with me, I can accomplish anything I want." He was saying, **"Because Christ strengthens me, I can be content and faithful regardless of my circumstance."**

---

## POINT 1 — CHRIST IS THE ANCHOR OF YOUR SOUL

**Hebrews 6:19 (NLT)**
> "This hope is a strong and trustworthy anchor for our souls. It leads us through the curtain into God's inner sanctuary."

The author of Hebrews teaches us that Christ is the anchor of the soul. And what is the soul? Your emotions. Your mind. Your thoughts.

So much of what people wrestle with today is framed as mental health, which is the state of the soul. But Scripture teaches that in order to have mental health, emotional stability, a heart that is humble and pure, **Christ has to be the anchor**.

Think about a boat. A boat without an anchor is always moved by the current. A boat without an anchor is always shifted by the waves, the winds, the rains, the storms. But when a boat has an anchor, it becomes a steadfast vessel.

And where is that anchor? **The anchor is underwater.** Which tells us this: *what is unseen must be stronger than what is seen.*

It does not matter how big the boat is. A cruise liner, a battleship, a cargo ship, a little canoe, a yacht. Expensive or cheap. New or old. **No matter the price, age, or size of the boat, without an anchor, it will drift.**

So Christ must be the anchor of our soul. Our bank accounts, our relationship statuses, our health, the things we face at work, the things we face in our nation — none of these have to determine who we are. **Christ is enough.**

---

## POINT 2 — WE LIVE THROUGH CHRIST BY ABIDING

If Paul says it is *through Christ* who strengthens me, then here is the question: how do we practically live through Christ? How do we make sure that every day our anchor is dropped and we become steadfast?

Jesus tells us himself.

**John 15:5-13**
> "I am the vine, you are the branches. He who abides in Me, and I in him, bears much fruit; for without Me you can do nothing. If anyone does not abide in Me, he is cast out as a branch and is withered; and they gather them and throw them into the fire, and they are burned. If you abide in Me, and My words abide in you, you will ask what you desire, and it shall be done for you. By this My Father is glorified, that you bear much fruit; so you will be My disciples. As the Father loved Me, I also have loved you; abide in My love. If you keep My commandments, you will abide in My love, just as I have kept My Father's commandments and abide in His love. These things I have spoken to you, that My joy may remain in you, and that your joy may be full. This is My commandment, that you love one another as I have loved you. Greater love has no one than this, than to lay down one's life for his friends."

Jesus gives us a metaphor. *I am the vine, you are the branches.* And branches are purposed to do what? **To bear fruit.**

So when we are not bearing the fruit we should bear, when we are not producing what we know is our potential, when we are not tasting the sweetness of the fruit in different areas of our life, Jesus is not saying, *"Try harder."* He is saying **something is wrong in your connection to Me**.

How does a branch bear fruit? Not through effort. Not through IQ. It bears fruit by connecting to the source of nutrients. It is the trunk, the vine, that brings the minerals from the soil and the water up into the branches. **The fruit is already in the branch, but its connection to the vine determines whether that fruit is produced.**

So Jesus is not saying do more. He is saying **abide more. Connect more. Remain in Me.**

---

## POINT 3 — ABIDE IN ME

The first place Jesus says to abide is in Himself. **"Abide in Me."**

We abide through fellowship with the Holy Spirit, by walking in the Spirit, being led by the Spirit, worshipping in Spirit, living in communion with God. For the Lord is Spirit.

**2 Corinthians 3:17**
> "Now the Lord is the Spirit; and where the Spirit of the Lord is, there is liberty."

**Romans 11:36**
> "For of Him and through Him and to Him are all things, to whom be glory forever. Amen."

**Acts 17:28**
> "For in Him we live and move and have our being."

---

## POINT 4 — ABIDE IN MY WORD

The second place Jesus says to abide is in His word. **"Abide in My word."**

**Psalm 1:2-3**
> "But his delight is in the law of the Lord, and in His law he meditates day and night. He shall be like a tree planted by the rivers of water, that brings forth its fruit in its season."

The blessed man meditates in the law of the Lord day and night, and he becomes like a tree planted by the water, producing fruit in every season.

**John 8:31-32**
> "If you abide in My word, you are My disciples indeed. And you shall know the truth, and the truth shall make you free."

How does the truth set you free? By **doing** what it says — abiding in His word.

**Joshua 1:8**
> "This Book of the Law shall not depart from your mouth, but you shall meditate in it day and night, that you may observe to do according to all that is written in it."

Before Joshua led the great conquest to attain the promise, God did not give him a military strategy. He gave him a simple instruction: keep the word in your mouth, meditate on it day and night. **Joshua attained the promise because he was abiding in the word.**

---

## POINT 5 — ABIDE IN MY LOVE

The third place Jesus says to abide is in His love. **"Abide in My love. If you keep My commandments, you will abide in My love."**

And how do we abide in His love? Jesus is not merely talking about loving Him in the abstract. He says, **"You love Me by loving one another."** Greater love has no one than this, than to lay down one's life for his friends.

This is the ultimate progression. Yes, abide in Me. Then abide in My word. But that abiding must become an action. **It becomes an expression of loving one another.**

We might not have silver and gold, as Peter and John said, but we can give people what we do have — **the love of Christ**. We abide in His love by laying down our lives.

---

## POINT 6 — THE INVITATION: COME AND LEARN

Here is Jesus' invitation to us.

**Matthew 11:28-30**
> "Come to Me, all you who labor and are heavy laden, and I will give you rest. Take My yoke upon you and learn from Me, for I am gentle and lowly in heart, and you will find rest for your souls. For My yoke is easy and My burden is light."

Listen to who Jesus is speaking to. Not simply those who labor *or* those who are heavy laden — **those who labor AND are heavy laden**. He is talking to people who are working but exhausted. People who are trying but overwhelmed. People putting in the effort but feeling empty, dry, and constantly tempted to quit.

He is speaking to people who are functioning externally but are empty on the inside. Doing things out of duty while the passion and the life no longer flow within them.

And what does Jesus say to that person? He does not say, *"Suck it up and try harder."* He does not say, *"You just need to do better."* He says, **"Come to Me."**

This is the gospel message from Genesis to Revelation. **Come to Me.** He is the Savior.

**Isaiah 63:1**
> "Who is this who comes from Edom, with dyed garments from Bozrah, this One who is glorious in His apparel, traveling in the greatness of His strength? — 'I who speak in righteousness, mighty to save.'"

When Isaiah saw the Redeemer, He came with garments drenched in the blood of the enemies of Israel, declaring, *"I am mighty to save."* Why would Jesus invite you to come to Him? **Because He is the one who gives salvation.** He is mightier than the things that feel like they are holding you back.

Then Jesus says something interesting. **"Learn from Me."** What has Paul been teaching us at the end of his life, writing from prison? *"I have learned the secret."* And now Jesus says, *"Come and learn."* There is no condemnation for getting it wrong — come to Me so I can show you how to do this the right way.

**Come. And learn.** Those are the two actions Jesus gives us.

---

## POINT 7 — THE ONE SIN THAT RESISTS THE INVITATION

If it is this simple — come and learn — why do we resist coming to Him? Why don't we daily throw ourselves at His feet?

There is one sin constantly fighting against coming to God. It is the age-old sin, the first sin ever recorded. It is **pride**.

Pride does not say God does not exist. **Pride says God may exist, but I can live apart from Him.** Pride says I might believe in Him, but I still believe in my own strength. I believe He is there, but I believe I can do this my own way.

This is why the famous passage on national healing begins where it begins.

**2 Chronicles 7:14**
> "If My people who are called by My name will humble themselves, and pray and seek My face, and turn from their wicked ways, then I will hear from heaven, and will forgive their sin and heal their land."

There is a progression here that ends in national healing. But notice the very first step. Before the praying, before the seeking, before the turning — **"humble themselves."** Humble yourself, and then pray.

A heart with the root of pride does not want to surrender. It whispers to its own mind, *"It will be okay. We can manage without prayer."*

**James 4:6-8**
> "But He gives more grace. Therefore He says: 'God resists the proud, but gives grace to the humble.' Therefore submit to God. Resist the devil and he will flee from you. Draw near to God and He will draw near to you. Cleanse your hands, you sinners; and purify your hearts, you double-minded."

It does not say God gives grace to those who attend church, or to those who fulfill their Christian duty. You can be inside the structures of church and still be resisted by God. **God resists the proud, but He gives grace to the humble.** When we humble ourselves, that is when grace is activated in our lives.

---

## POINT 8 — RUN TO THE NAME AND BE SAFE

When we return to a place of abiding in Him, we realize again that Jesus is mighty to save.

**Proverbs 18:10**
> "The name of the Lord is a strong tower; the righteous run to it and are safe."

Hear this carefully. It does not say the righteous are perfect, that they get it all right and have it all together. **It describes the righteous as those who run to the name of the Lord.** That is where they find their salvation. That is where they are safe.

Whatever you are facing — whatever battles, struggles, situations, whatever intimidates you or leaves you nervous — know that when you call upon the name of the Lord, **God is not intimidated by the mountain in front of you**. The mountain that scares you trembles before Him.

What is the satisfied life? It is **learning to take everything I need to take, to Jesus**. To call on His name. To trust that whatever is going on in our lives, He is our salvation, our strong tower, our fortress, the one who hides us under His mighty wings. No matter what we face, we can call upon His name.

## CONCLUSION & CALL TO ACTION

Maybe you fall into this category today — the category of laboring and being heavy laden, burdened. And today Jesus is inviting you again.

*"My son, my daughter, just come to Me. Let Me teach you how to do marriage the right way. Let Me teach you how to be a businessman in My ways. How to be a father, a mother, how to be a leader, a disciple. How to be influential in your community, how to be the light, how to operate in broken environments. Let Me teach you — because living on the outside while being heavy laden on the inside is not My design."*

Peter tells us to cast our cares upon the Lord, because He cares for us. So give Him your burdens.

> *"Father, we come before You right now. We give You every burden, all our trouble, all our care. We humble ourselves and we return to You. Forgive us where we have been full of pride. Cleanse us with the blood of Jesus. Create in us a clean heart and a steadfast spirit. As we draw close to You, draw close to us. Restore the joy of Your salvation. Give us beauty for ashes, the oil of joy for mourning, the garment of praise for the spirit of heaviness. We call upon Your name, Jesus — the only name that saves. Thank You that You are the source of our lives, that You give us rest for our souls, and that Your peace guards our hearts and our minds. In Jesus' name. Amen."*

## SALVATION CALL

If you have never given your life to Jesus, this is your invitation right now. If you have never turned away from your sin and surrendered to the Lord, never put your faith in what Jesus did for you on the cross of Calvary — where He was punished for your sin so that you could receive forgiveness and a new life — this is your opportunity to make a decision to surrender to Christ. He is calling you to come.

And maybe you have backslidden. You have disconnected from the Lord, and everything has become dead, cold, and ritualistic. But you say, *"I need a miracle. I need the mercy of God. I need to throw myself at the feet of Jesus. I want to be born again, revived and resurrected in Him."* This is your moment. Don't wait for tomorrow. The time of salvation is right now.

**Salvation Prayer:**

> *"Lord Jesus, here I am. Today I decide to surrender to You, to answer Your call, and to put my life in Your hands. Thank You, Lord, that You come into my life and transform me. Fill me with Your Spirit. Make me a new creation. Thank You, Lord, that You forgive me from all my sin. You cleanse me, and You give me a new heart. And I know, as of this day, by faith I am a child of God. By Your grace, I am born again. And I confess that Jesus is the Son of God, who came in the flesh, who died on the cross, who rose again, and who is coming back for me. Thank You, Lord, that I am Yours and You are mine, forevermore. In Jesus' name. Amen."*
