# DISCIPLESHIP PRINCIPLES
**Speaker:** Hendrik Vorster
**Date:** 2026-07-26
**Source:** 3C Church

---

## INTRODUCTION

What really brings glory to God is not us just singing, "Glorify your name." What really glorifies the Lord is **bearing much fruit.**

> **John 15:8**
> "This is to my Father's glory, that you bear much fruit, showing yourself to be my disciples."

Jesus spoke to his disciples about treasures in heaven. Treasures in heaven is not monetary things. Treasures in heaven is **souls.**

> **Matthew 6:19-21**
> "Do not store up for yourselves treasures on earth, where moth and vermin destroy, and where thieves break in and steal, but store up for yourselves treasures in heaven, where moths and vermin do not destroy, where thieves do not break in and steal. For where your treasure is, your heart will be also."

God wants you and I to value souls. God wants you and I to build a heavenly account of souls — people that you and I led to the Lord.

---

## POINT 1 — DISCIPLESHIP STARTS WITH A PERSONAL COMMITMENT TO FOLLOW JESUS

No discipleship is possible unless you and I personally follow in the footsteps of Jesus.

> **Matthew 4:19**
> "Come after me," Jesus said, "and I will make you fishers of men."

All of us want to be fishers of men. But the starting point of becoming a fisher of men is that you and I need to start **following Jesus every day.**

It is an intentional approach of following Jesus every day of our life. That's what God wants us to do — to follow Him in His footsteps and to be taught of the Lord every day.

> **John 13:15**
> "I have set you an example that you should do as I have done for you."

One of the amazing things we learn when we follow Christ is that He gave us an example to follow. And He expresses a desire that He wants us to do as He did for us.

---

## POINT 2 — DISCIPLESHIP ONLY BECOMES REAL WHEN YOU BECOME AN EXAMPLE FOR OTHERS TO FOLLOW

Discipleship is not just a class. Discipleship is not a department. Discipleship is not a course. **Discipleship is a lifestyle.** Me becoming an example for others to follow. That is what discipleship is about.

People don't follow what you tell them. **They follow what they see is true in your life.**

You and I need to model that through the spiritual disciplines that we practice:

- We fast
- We pray
- We read the word
- We witness
- We share our faith
- We practice the presence of God
- We worship — the first thing we do in the mornings

But it's also the example of the **character of Christ** that we live. Jesus taught his disciples values. Values of the kingdom of God. Every kingdom has values:

- We love
- We forgive
- We are gracious
- We are trustworthy
- We are reliable
- We are loyal
- We are faithful
- We are kind
- We are peacemakers

It is those values that changed the early culture in Jesus' day to such an extent that even the Roman Empire, who was hostile towards Christ, could not but change — because they observed **these men have been with Jesus.**

We need to make a commitment: I'm going to be an example for my parents, for my children, for my spouse, for the people I work with — of what it means to be a true follower walking in the footsteps of Jesus.

> **1 Corinthians 11:1**
> "Follow my example as I follow the example of Christ."

What a statement. Follow my example.

> **Philippians 3:17**
> "Join with others in following my example, and take note of those who live according to the pattern we gave you."

There's a pattern and there's an example. If we want to see this nation and nations of the earth changed, we need to make a commitment to be examples so that they can see what that life looks like.

> **Philippians 4:9**
> "Whatever you have learned or received or heard from me, or seen in me — put it into practice, and the God of peace will be with you."

People follow your example, not what you say, not what you tell them. **They follow what they see and observe in your life.**

---

## POINT 3 — MAKING DISCIPLES IS THE PROCESS OF TEACHING BELIEVERS TO OBEY AND APPLY

Making disciples defines the process by which we teach and equip believers to obey and apply the things they've learned from the Holy Spirit and what they observe in our lives.

> **Matthew 28:19-20**
> "Go make disciples of all nations, baptize them... and teach them to obey everything that I have commanded you."

It is that obedience to the principles and teachings of Jesus that makes us rise up. When we disciple people we've led to the Lord, it is also empowering them and helping them to put the gifts of God into practice.

God promised through the word that in the last days He will pour out His Spirit on all flesh. The Holy Spirit comes inside of us for a purpose — that God can use you and me as His instruments by the power of the Spirit, by the gifts of God's Spirit, to change people's lives.

It is that prophetic word. It is that word of knowledge. It is that word of wisdom. It is that word God puts in your heart that you speak into somebody's life and it changes them.

That day Jesus was walking by the Lake of Gennesaret. He saw two boats. He got into the one belonging to Simon Peter — who was an unbeliever at that point. But when a miracle took place in front of him, in that moment he fell on his knees and said, "Go away from me. I'm a sinner." Because he witnessed firsthand a miracle take place in his life.

**God wants you and I to walk in the power of the Spirit.** As disciple makers, we need to help people connect with the Holy Spirit inside of them — to be taught of the Lord, to listen to the Lord, to yield to the Lord, and to see miracles happen around them.

> **John 14:26**
> "But the Counselor, the Holy Spirit, whom the Father will send in my name, will teach you all things and will remind you of everything that I said to you."

The Holy Spirit is that one ingredient in your life that makes all the difference. We are born again by the Spirit of the Lord. He makes us a new creation. He is our comforter. He's with us every single day of our lives.

> **1 John 2:27**
> "As for you, the anointing you received from him remains in you."

You have received a deposit from God by the Holy Spirit. The Holy Spirit came and dwelled inside of each and every one of us as believers. The purpose for the Spirit of God is to teach us. The more we yield to the Holy Spirit, the more we find that He teaches us, He guides us, He directs us, He empowers us, He gives us strength to do extraordinary things for Him and for His name's sake.

---

## POINT 4 — THE GOAL OF DISCIPLESHIP IS TO SEEK AND SAVE THE LOST

The goal of discipleship is to lead people to Christ, to seek and save the lost, to disciple those whom we lead to the Lord.

> **Luke 19:10**
> "For the Son of Man came to seek and save the lost."

> **Matthew 4:19**
> "Follow me and I will make you fishers of men."

We want to fish men. We want to seek and save lost people around us. There are so many lost people all around us. God wants you and I to be those instruments through whom we can seek and save lost people.

> **Daniel 12:3**
> "Those who are wise will shine like the brightness of the heavens, and those who lead many to righteousness, like the stars forever and ever."

I want to be that wise person. I want to be that bright shining star. How? **By leading many to righteousness.**

Everywhere you and I go, there's an opportunity to lead somebody to Christ. We need to seize the opportunity to lead people to Christ. But how will these people be led to the Lord unless you and I go? Unless you and I obey the call of God.

In Acts 9, Ananias was praying and the Lord spoke to him: "I want you to go down to Straight Street and lay hands on Saul." Ananias — the Bible didn't describe him as an apostle, as a pastor. He was a **disciple.** God found in him an instrument that He could send.

In Acts 8, Philip was praying. The Lord says, "Philip, I want you to go down to the street going to Gaza." Immediately Philip went. When he came there, the Spirit spoke to him: "Now go to that chariot and stay close to it." He ran over. He heard a man reading from the scroll of Isaiah. He was so well established in the word — because he was a disciple — that he could recognize the scriptures being read. He said, "Do you understand what you are reading?" The man said, "How can I, if nobody explains it to me?" He led him to the Lord and baptized him that day.

Why? Because he was a disciple. He was obedient. **He could be sent.**

## CONCLUSION & CALL TO ACTION

God is asking you the same question He asked Isaiah:

> **Isaiah 6:8**
> "Then I heard the voice of the Lord saying, 'Whom shall I send? And who will go for us?' And I said, 'Here am I. Send me.'"

That call is in our house this morning. *"Whom can I send? Whom can I send to that street? Who can I send to that house? Whom can I send to that man, to that woman? Who will go for me?"*

The Lord is asking you.

**I will go. Send me. I will go. Send me.**

*Father, I pray for every man and woman standing this morning. You want us to go. You want us to go and preach the gospel. You want us to share our faith. I pray that You will find in them men and women who will go. Men and women who can be sent — even this afternoon. That they will be obedient servants, obedient to Your Holy Spirit, to be sent. I pray for this in the precious name of Jesus. Use these men and women. Send them, and use them, that many people will come to know You as their Lord and Savior.*
