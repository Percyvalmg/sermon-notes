## INTRODUCTION

When we look around our nation, it is very clear to see that our families are under pressure. Grandparents are carrying the responsibility of raising grandchildren. Financial burdens, broken relationships, violence, addiction, and the busyness of modern life continue to strain our homes.

These may be the facts, **but an even greater truth prevails.**

**God has not given up on the family.** He is the God of the family. He loves your family.

Throughout the scriptures, whenever we see God's people flourish, it was because they kept God at the center of their lives and their homes. God cannot be an afterthought or an addition to our lives — **he must be first.** He must be the center of our home.

**Families that remember the Lord will thrive.**

---

## POINT 1 — OUR HOMES SHOULD BE TRAINING GROUNDS, NOT BATTLE ZONES

> **Psalm 144:1**
> "Blessed be the Lord, my rock, who trains my hands for war and my fingers for battle."

We are in a war. The Bible says God trains us. He trains our hands for war and our fingers for battle. **Our homes should be training grounds, not battle zones.**

A lot of times we look at our spouse and find them to be the enemy. That's when you know the enemy has distracted you from your calling and your purpose as a family. If there's infighting in the family, if there's division, if there's unforgiveness — this place that is supposed to be a training ground will not be effective or flourish.

As families, we need to be intentional about:
- Building a habitation for the presence of God
- Dealing with things that cause division
- Having difficult conversations
- Fostering a culture of forgiveness in our households

> **Mark 3:25**
> "And if a house is divided against itself, that house cannot stand."

Your spouse is not the enemy. Your children are not the enemy. Your parents are not the enemy. **Together is better.**

---

## POINT 2 — WE TRAIN THROUGH OUR EVERYDAY INTERACTIONS

> **Deuteronomy 6:6–7**
> "And you must commit yourselves wholeheartedly to these commands that I am giving you today. Repeat them again and again to your children. Talk about them when you are at home and when you are on the road, when you are going to bed and when you are getting up."

There is always an opportunity for us to learn — parents learning from children and children learning from parents. There is always an opportunity to teach and to repeat the principles of the word.

Here's the challenge. We have to ask ourselves:
- What do we talk about when we're driving in the car with our kids?
- What do our kids hear us talk about?
- What do they hear you say when you're watching a game?
- What do they hear you say when you speak about the president?
- What rhetoric have we been piggybacking on where our children hear us express our opinions about the state of the country?
- What faith do we want them to have when they listen to the opinions we have about our nation?

When you are sitting with your children, take opportunities to bring up scenarios they may face. For instance — what would they say if someone tells them, *"Your religion is actually a false religion"*? How will they be able to stand?

**We need to prepare our children to face the anti-God culture that we find in society.** Where our children are forced to be muzzled, not allowed to freely express that they are Christians. Where they are seen as overbearing or told they should not be expressing themselves.

It is important that we are intentional to prepare our children to handle the resistances they will face in their daily lives.

---

## POINT 3 — OBEDIENCE IN THE HOME TEACHES REVERENCE FOR GOD

> **Psalm 34:11**
> "Come, you children, listen to me. I will teach you the fear of the Lord."

A child who is allowed to not heed instructions from parents is being set up for a **lack of reverence for God and his instructions.**

When our children see us obey — when we teach our children to obey his word as they listen to us — we teach them to be able to obey God's word when he speaks.

We train by modeling. We train through the words we speak. We train by ensuring there is time for the word of God to be read and for prayers as a family.

**It is critical that there is a time established in every household for the word and for prayer.**

---

## POINT 4 — PERSONAL DEVOTION BUILDS STRENGTH IN EACH MEMBER OF THE FAMILY

Every member of the family must cultivate a personal devotional life. From the youngest child to the eldest — from the time they can speak, it is important to establish them having a personal time with God.

What personal devotion looks like:
- Wake up and begin with prayer and thanksgiving
- Read the word of God, asking him to reveal it to you
- Take notes and write down how to apply it in daily life
- Close in prayer

What happens when you skip devotion? You go into your day by yourself. You become moody, easily angered, and carry a negative mindset. But when you are in the word, the Holy Spirit is there — speaking, guiding, and giving you purpose for the day.

---

## POINT 5 — FAMILY CELL IS WHERE THE FAMILY COLLECTIVELY REMEMBERS THE LORD

> **Joshua 24:15**
> "As for me and my house, we will serve the Lord."

Family cell is a place where the family unit can collectively remember the Lord — remember who he is, what he has done, and what he has spoken.

**The structure of family cell:**

1. **Thanksgiving** — Go from one family member to the next: "What are you thankful to God for?"
2. **The Word** — Share a scripture or devotion together. In every word, find a promise to keep and a command to obey.
3. **Forgiveness** — Ask, "Who do you need to forgive?" Create space for confession and reconciliation.
4. **Communion** — Take communion together as a family. Bring your family to the foot of the cross. Our families are only as strong as our ability to get to Jesus.
5. **Prayer & Declaration** — Pray together and declare the word of God over your household.

It doesn't have to be perfect. It can be challenging, especially with little ones. Be patient in the process. What matters is getting into the word of God and praying together — that is what brings unity in the home.

If you are a child introducing family cell, don't force your parents to repent. Be wise. Create an atmosphere where everybody can freely engage one another.

## POINT 6 — THE PROMISE FOR FAMILIES WHO REMEMBER

> **Psalm 144:12–15**
> "May our sons flourish in their youth like well-nurtured plants. May our daughters be like graceful pillars carved to beautify a palace. May our barns be filled with crops of every kind. May the flocks in our fields multiply by the thousands, even to the ten thousands. And may our oxen be loaded down with produce. May there be no enemy breaking through our walls, no going into captivity, no cries of alarm in our town squares. Yes, joyful are those who live like this. Joyful indeed are those whose God is the Lord."

This is what we trust God for — that our homes will be:
- A place of joy
- A place where the presence of God is
- A place where every single member flourishes

Maybe you haven't been faithful to do this. Maybe you had started and you're not doing family cell anymore. **You can start again.** There is an opportunity to start again and cultivate a culture of dependence on God.

He's not mad at you. Matter of fact, he's madly in love with you.
