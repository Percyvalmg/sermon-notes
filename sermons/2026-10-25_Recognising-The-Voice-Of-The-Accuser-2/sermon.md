## INTRODUCTION

We are continuing this new series on **recognising the voice of the accuser**.

Has this helped you over the past week? Were you more in tune with the Holy Spirit? More aware of the enemy and his tactics? More accurate in living out your faith?

Our key scripture again:

> **Revelation 12:10**
> "...for the accuser of our brethren is cast down, which accused them before our God day and night."

The brethren — and of course that includes all of humanity. If you are human, there is a force against you. If you are human, there is an enemy. If you are human, there is a strategist plotting to kill, steal, and destroy.

And he is not busy some of the time. **He accuses you day and night.**

In the spirit there is no sleep. There is no slumber. There is no tired in heaven, because a spirit being does not need the rest the physical body needs. And so Satan, and every demonic power, is opposing you and accusing you day and night.

But the good news is how the verse ends. **He has been defeated. He has been cast down.** He may be doing this, but if we know how to tap into what God has done, it should not affect us.

That is why we need to learn to recognise when it is the enemy and when it is God speaking to us.

### Every Voice Has a Source

There are only two sources. On your phone it may look like there are hundreds of sources, because you have hundreds of apps. But **everything is either born of God or born of darkness.** Everything.

There is no neutral. There is no objective. There is no place where it doesn't lean to one side or the other. **It is of God or it is not.**

- We need to know when it is God — so that we may act, obey, and run with the vision.
- We need to know when it is not — so that we can renounce, take captive, pull down, stand against, and bind.

### Every Voice Has a Character

It either is pure and holy — accurate, encouraging, empowering, liberating, full of light. Or it carries the character of the evil one — cruel, malicious, destructive, and all kinds of dark.

### Every Voice Has an Intent

Maybe most important of all: **there is intent behind every voice** that speaks over your life, into your mind, into your heart, into your marriage, into your family.

**Nothing is random.** We do not live by fate or luck or coincidence. We live by intent.

Everything you listen to on the radio, everything you lend your ear to on television, everything you allow into your eye through social media and the hours on your phone — all of it has an intent. It has a destination. It has a purpose.

And if we do not discern, we could be sucked in, ensnared, taken captive by that which is not of God.

But praise the Lord, He did not leave us to our own devices. **It's not about intellect. It's not about education. It's not about my humanity.** It is about the Spirit that lives in me — the Spirit of truth that says, *"This is right. This is light. This is God."*

So which voice are we listening to? **The accuser or the restorer?**

- The accuser — Satan and his demonic kingdom — condemns, divides, and captures.
- The voice of Jesus convicts, restores, liberates, heals, and saves. It lifts up, builds up, encourages, and empowers.

### Recap of Last Week

1. **The devil is called the accuser.** The name *diabolos* literally means slanderer. Slanderer, accuser, devil — one and the same.
2. **When we slander, we participate in the spirit of the devil.** When we speak ill, when we engage in talk that does not glorify God, we are participating in the spirit of Satan himself.
3. **The devil accuses, but Jesus restores.** The accuser wants to break down, kill, steal, and destroy. Jesus' ministry is a ministry of restoration.
4. **A form of godliness without power.** Some people sound spiritual but have no power to transform a life. Their motives are impure and their hearts are far from God.
5. **From such people turn away.** Don't try to change them. Don't debate them. Don't argue with them. They are not open to hear what you have to say.

> **2 Corinthians 5:18**
> "And all things are of God, who hath reconciled us to himself by Jesus Christ, and hath given to us the ministry of reconciliation."

You have not been called to the ministry of accusation. **You have been called to the ministry of reconciliation.**

*"But it's true."* It doesn't matter if it is true. What is your objective with that truth? What is the motive of your heart behind exposing what happened? Do you want that truth to set free, liberate, and bring repentance — or do you want to humiliate, embarrass, and hurt?

> **2 Timothy 3:5**
> "Having a form of godliness, but denying the power thereof: from such turn away."

Today we look at **the purpose of the devil in his accusation.**

## POINT 6 — THE ACCUSER WANTS TO WEAKEN YOU, BUT JESUS PRAYS YOU STRONG

The first purpose of the accuser is to **weaken the believer.** To rob you of your strength.

When he accuses you, he has every intention, every motive, every plan to strip you of the power of God in your life and leave you weak.

But Jesus is praying the opposite over your life. Right now He is making intercession for you to be strong in the Lord and in the power of His might — and having done everything, to be found standing.

> **Ephesians 6:10, 13**
> "Finally, my brethren, be strong in the Lord, and in the power of his might... and having done all, to stand."

### Satan Asked for Peter by Name

Have you had those days where you felt like the devil called your name? Where he asked for you specifically?

> **Luke 22:31–32**
> "And the Lord said, Simon, Simon, behold, Satan hath desired to have you, that he may sift you as wheat: but I have prayed for thee, that thy faith fail not: and when thou art converted, strengthen thy brethren."

He came for Peter by name. He had a plan. He had a purpose. He intended to destroy Peter, rob him of the power of God, and leave him weak and defeated.

If it were up to us, we would hide Peter somewhere. *"No, devil. You cannot have him. There will be no sifting."*

But Jesus doesn't hide him.

*Jesus, did you hear what he asked? He wants to destroy me. He wants to kill me. He wants to steal from me. He wants to leave me without power. He wants to sift me like wheat and cast me aside.*

Why would Jesus not take you out of that situation? Why would He not quench the fire? Why would He not stay the flood? Why does He say, *"In the fire I will be with you. Through the flood it will be Me and you"*?

> **Isaiah 43:2**
> "When thou passest through the waters, I will be with thee; and through the rivers, they shall not overflow thee: when thou walkest through the fire, thou shalt not be burned."

**Because as the enemy sifts you, Jesus is making intercession for you.** He is pleading with the Father:

*"Father, let's use this to Your glory. Let's use this to magnify Your name. Let's show the enemy how much he has been defeated. Let's strengthen this one. Let's equip, steady, and affirm this one."*

Jesus doesn't remove the sifting. He doesn't remove the enemy. **You are accused day and night — but Jesus is praying for you day and night.** Every minute of every day.

*Strengthen her, Father. Strengthen her faith. Strengthen her resolve. May the resurrection power of Jesus work in her. Let her stand, Lord. And as she is being sifted, bring glory to Your name.*

> **Romans 8:34**
> "Who is he that condemneth? It is Christ that died, yea rather, that is risen again, who is even at the right hand of God, who also maketh intercession for us."

There is a power at work that is greater than the accusations of Satan — **the power of the prayers of Christ.**

### Strengthen Your Brethren

As Peter was strengthened, you and I can be strengthened by the prayers of Jesus. We will not be weakened. **We will be strengthened in the fire.**

And when you come out the other side — *strengthen your brethren.* Now you have a testimony. Now you have a witness. Now you have a weapon.

The one who thought he was going to destroy you **misjudged you.** He misjudged the One standing beside you. He misjudged the prayers of the redeeming King.

You are not only going to come through — you are going to come through with a greater arsenal, greater weaponry, greater power.

**The devil may be talking about you, but Jesus is praying for you.**

So when we see someone going through the most — when we see them being sifted — what should we do? **Be like Jesus.**

- Don't accuse.
- Don't point fingers.
- Don't question.
- Don't be suspicious.
- Make intercession and pray.

**Strengthen. Strengthen. Strengthen.**

## POINT 7 — THE ACCUSER WANTS TO SHAME YOU, BUT GOD CLOTHES YOU IN RIGHTEOUSNESS

The second purpose of the accuser is to **humiliate you.** To shame you.

What happens when we are humiliated and ashamed? **We hide.** We crawl into the shadows and become ineffective. No one can find us, because we are embarrassed. We are made low.

### Joshua the High Priest

> **Zechariah 3:1–4**
> "Then he showed me Joshua the high priest standing before the Angel of the LORD, and Satan standing at his right hand to oppose him. And the LORD said to Satan, 'The LORD rebuke you, Satan! The LORD who has chosen Jerusalem rebuke you! Is this not a brand plucked from the fire?' Now Joshua was clothed with filthy garments, and was standing before the Angel. Then He answered and spoke to those who stood before Him, saying, 'Take away the filthy garments from him.' And to him He said, 'See, I have removed your iniquity from you, and I will clothe you with rich robes.'"

The Angel of the Lord is written with a capital — Joshua is standing before Jesus. And in the same space, Satan comes and stands at his right hand **to oppose him.**

And the Lord says, *"The Lord rebuke you, Satan."*

I want the Lord to speak on my behalf. I want God to open His mouth in my defence. I want heaven to advocate my case. **The Lord** — not me, not Joshua, not the high priest. *The Lord rebuke you.*

### The Accusations Were True

Joshua was clothed with filthy garments. Which means **the accusations were true.** This was not an innocent man.

He was representing the entire nation of Israel. Garments in the Bible represent the state of the being. Filthy garments indicate sin, defilement, evil, and darkness. And he is standing for all the people in the presence of God with filthy garments.

**And God does not chase him. God does not rebuke Joshua. God rebukes Satan.**

What does Satan point out? The filthy garments. The defilement. The evil. The sin.

*I know. I know I am a failure. I know I have flaws and weaknesses and sins. I know there is darkness. You don't have to tell me. I am fully aware of my humanity.*

But instead of humiliating and shaming Joshua, see what God answers: **"Take away the filthy garments. I remove your iniquity from you, and I clothe you with rich robes."**

*I know you have sinned. I know you have fallen short. I know your heart is corrupt. I know all the things you have done, said, and desired — and they are filthy. But I shed My blood so that I could wash you clean, purify you, and cleanse you.*

As He said to Joshua, as He said to Israel, He says to you: **your iniquity has been taken away, and you have been clothed with robes of righteousness.**

> **2 Corinthians 5:21**
> "For he hath made him to be sin for us, who knew no sin; that we might be made the righteousness of God in him."

**We are the righteousness of God in Christ.**

I am not in denial of who I was when He found me. I am not pretending to be something I was not. But by the grace of God, by the mercy of God, by the compassion, kindness, and power of God — **I am no longer who I was.**

> **2 Corinthians 5:17**
> "Therefore if any man be in Christ, he is a new creature: old things are passed away; behold, all things are become new."

I have been washed in the blood of the Lamb. I have been cleansed. My iniquity has been wiped away. **I stand whiter than snow.**

### Cover, Don't Expose

I will not humiliate people. I will not shame people when I see their sin.

> **Genesis 9:22–23**
> "And Ham, the father of Canaan, saw the nakedness of his father, and told his two brethren without. And Shem and Japheth took a garment, and laid it upon both their shoulders, and went backward, and covered the nakedness of their father."

Some say Noah's drunkenness was a mistake. It wasn't a mistake. He knew. But **one son exposed, and the others covered.**

It didn't make what Noah did right. But it is a spirit that says: *we all fall, we all fail, we are all fragile.* We're not going to deny it — but we're going to allow the blood to speak. We're going to allow the cross to work. We're going to allow the Spirit of God to wash away iniquity and restore purity and righteousness.

**Satan points to your filth and says, "Look at this darkness." Jesus points to the cross and says, "Look at the righteousness."**

Look what the grace of God can do.

## POINT 8 — THE ACCUSER WANTS TO CONDEMN YOU, BUT THE HOLY SPIRIT CONVICTS UNTO CHANGE

The third purpose of the accuser is to **condemn you.**

To condemn means **to put in a state of hopelessness.**

- There is nothing you can do.
- It's too late.
- There is nothing you can give or pay.
- There is nothing you can say to turn this around.
- It is over.

Condemnation says there is a worthlessness about you and no hope for your future.

**It has never been the intention of God to allow you to be condemned.**

### Conviction, Not Condemnation

Does that mean God ignores sin? No.

God confronts sin through His Holy Spirit. It's called **the conviction of the Holy Spirit.**

You *should* feel bad when the Holy Spirit speaks to you. But it shouldn't condemn you. It shouldn't make you feel worthless, insignificant, and hopeless. It should bring you to the place where you say, *"Really? That was in me? That came out of me? God, have mercy. Lord, forgive me."*

Godly sorrow brings me to repentance, where I confess my sin and cry out for mercy.

> **2 Corinthians 7:10**
> "For godly sorrow worketh repentance to salvation not to be repented of: but the sorrow of the world worketh death."

> **John 16:8**
> "And when he is come, he will reprove the world of sin, and of righteousness, and of judgment."

**Convict. Not condemn.**

Conviction is good, because **there is no change without conviction.** But never condemnation.

### The Accuser's Game in Church

This is a game the devil plays in church. **He will take the very word of God and condemn you.**

*You can't be forgiven. You've done it too many times. If they knew what God knew, they would not allow you through these doors. You are unworthy. You are beyond redemption.*

But the blood says, **"Come."** The blood says, **"Come."** The blood says, **"Come and be changed."**

> **1 John 1:9**
> "If we confess our sins, he is faithful and just to forgive us our sins, and to cleanse us from all unrighteousness."

He doesn't only cancel the debt. He doesn't only remove what is owed. **He changes us from the inside out.** It is no longer who you are. It is no longer what you want. It is no longer where you go. It is no longer your label.

*I have convicted you. You have confessed. Now you are changed* — and you walk in the newness and likeness of Christ.

### Show the Same Grace

Don't condemn people. Don't say, *"We can never fix this. There is no hope. It is too late. It is too far. It is too bad."*

- In Christ there is always hope.
- In Christ there is always redemption.
- In Christ there is always deliverance.
- In Christ there is always a future.

## CONCLUSION & DECLARATION

Identify them. Discern them. We have a choice to make:

| The Voice of the Accuser | The Voice of Jesus |
|---|---|
| Accusations that weaken | Prayers that strengthen |
| Accusations that shame and humiliate | Forgiveness unto righteousness |
| Accusations that condemn | Conviction unto change |

First we get to experience the redemptive work of Christ. Then, in turn, **we get to be instruments of it** in the world where God has placed us.

> **Romans 8:1**
> "There is therefore now no condemnation to them which are in Christ Jesus, who walk not after the flesh, but after the Spirit."

As we are in Christ, **we are not condemned. We are not humiliated. We are not shamed. We are not weakened.**

Now we must apply the word we have heard. Ask yourself:

- Which voice am I listening to?
- Which voice am I allowing to plant within my heart?
- Which voice's intent am I aligning myself with?

The accuser says, *"Look what you did. Look who you are. Look what you said. Look where you've been. Look at your filthy robes."*

Jesus says, *"Come. Come be saved. Come be redeemed. Come be delivered. Come be healed. Come be restored. Come be set right."*

**The voice of Jesus always draws us to the Father.** Never does it push us away from God. Never does it make us cringe in a corner, afraid of God. The Holy Spirit draws us to the loving heart of our Father, where we may repent — and when we repent, we are changed into the likeness of Christ.

We must acknowledge that we have been operating in accusation. We have humiliated. We have shamed. We have weakened. We have condemned people — maybe even ourselves.

Maybe you've said, *"I am such rubbish. I am absolutely worthless. I am never going to change."*

Today there is a deliverer in the house. There is a healer in the house. **There is a restorer in the house.**

Put your hand on your heart and declare this together:

> *Father, in the name of Jesus, we reject the voice of the accuser.*
> *We reject the spirit of condemnation.*
> *We reject shame and hopelessness, and all weakness, in the name of Jesus.*
> *We declare that we are not defined by our failures.*
> *We are who You say we are.*
> *We are forgiven. We are redeemed. We are justified. We are restored.*
> *When the accuser points to our past, we will point to the cross.*
> *When the accuser condemns, Jesus makes intercession.*
> *When You convict us, give us the humility to repent.*
> *And when the enemy condemns us, give us the faith to reject him.*
> *There is now no condemnation, for we are in Christ.*
> *We will not partner with the accuser.*
> *We align ourselves, we position ourselves with Jesus, the Restorer —*
> *with His grace, His forgiveness, His love, and His mercy.*
> *We choose Jesus. Amen and amen.*

## SALVATION CALL

Coming to church doesn't make you a Christian. Having Christian parents doesn't make you a Christian. Giving to the offering doesn't make you a Christian. **You must be born again.**

> **John 3:6–7**
> "That which is born of the flesh is flesh; and that which is born of the Spirit is spirit. Marvel not that I said unto thee, Ye must be born again."

What is born of your mother and father makes you a child of the earth, a child of your parents. It doesn't make you a child of God. In the same manner you were born of your parents, **you must be born of God.**

It's not a physical thing. It's a spiritual thing — the Spirit of God comes and regenerates you. He takes out the old nature that loves sin. The old nature that loves accusation. The old nature that cannot help itself but slander. **And He gives you the nature of Jesus.**

> **Romans 10:9**
> "That if thou shalt confess with thy mouth the Lord Jesus, and shalt believe in thine heart that God hath raised him from the dead, thou shalt be saved."

If you've never confessed Him with your mouth, **today is your day.**

Maybe you've prayed that prayer before, but life has been so difficult. You've questioned Him. You've been suspicious of Him. You've drifted away. You find yourself on an ocean of doubt, unhappy with God because of what has happened in your life.

He is here today to bring you back to a place of faith — where you can see the future again, where you can have hope again for tomorrow. If you are backslidden and far from the Lord, **today is the day of salvation.**

> **2 Corinthians 6:2**
> "...behold, now is the accepted time; behold, now is the day of salvation."

Not tomorrow. Not next week. **Now** — because you don't know your tomorrow. Now you are hearing the word of God. Now there is faith in this room. Now the Spirit is here to save you.

Pray this with me:

> *Jesus, I come as I am,*
> *acknowledging my sin, confessing my failures,*
> *and I ask that You come and make me new.*
> *I confess that I believe You are the Son of God who died on a cross for my sin,*
> *and now resurrected, seated in heaven, You are praying for me.*
> *I ask that Your Holy Spirit will come and make me new.*
> *Make me a child of God.*
> *Wash me and cleanse me in Your precious blood.*
> *I am born again. I am a child of God. I live now for You.*
> *Amen and amen.*
