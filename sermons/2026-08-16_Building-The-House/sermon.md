# BUILDING THE HOUSE
**Speaker:** Marius Nel
**Date:** 2026-08-16
**Source:** 3C Church (3C Live Experience)

---

## INTRODUCTION

We are all building. We are building our families. We are building our marriages. We are building our nation. We are building the ministry God has given us as disciple makers.

The key text this morning is Psalm 127:1-5, and it gives us **God's template** — his way of building the house. Three things sit at the center of it, and God wants to cement them into our lives today.

> **Psalm 127:1-2**
> "Unless the Lord builds the house, they labor in vain who build it; unless the Lord guards the city, the watchman stays awake in vain. It is vain for you to rise up early, to sit up late, to eat the bread of sorrows; for so He gives His beloved sleep."

Building in vain ends in shame. Everybody builds — but for some, their lives end in shame, and for others, their lives glorify God. The difference is not whether you build. The difference is **who builds it, and by what template**.

---

## POINT 1 — GOD IS THE MASTER BUILDER

> **Hebrews 3:4**
> "For every house is built by someone, but He who built all things is God."

Every house has a builder. The question is: who is building your house? On what foundation are you building your life? You can build for decades — you can build your entire life — and still end up in shame. There is a way that seems right to a man, but its end is destruction.

God is the Master Builder. God is the architect. God is the one who drew up the plans.

We see this in Exodus 25, where God gave Moses the template for the tabernacle — the drawings, the detail — and said: *build everything according to my specification. Don't miss anything. Be accurate. I want to dwell with my people.*

Jesus received that same kind of template from the Father and passed it to His disciples: *"Go into all the world. Make disciples. Have arrows in your quiver."*

> **Matthew 28:19-20**
> "Go therefore and make disciples of all the nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit, teaching them to observe all things that I have commanded you; and lo, I am with you always, even to the end of the age."

God is not only the architect — He is the contractor. He lays the foundation. He does the work.

> **1 Corinthians 3:9**
> "For we are God's fellow workers; you are God's field, you are God's building."

We can only say we are working *with* God if we are building on His foundation.

> **1 Corinthians 3:11**
> "For no other foundation can anyone lay than that which is laid, which is Jesus Christ."

It is wonderful to sing that Christ is our firm foundation. We can sing it — we can even shed a tear over it. But the question is: is He *actually* your foundation, or do you have a different one? The foundation says, *go and make disciples, baptize them, teach them — and I will be with you.* That is the template that keeps you from shame.

---

## POINT 2 — THE TEMPLATE IS COMPLETE, AND CANNOT BE IMPROVED

> **Ecclesiastes 3:14**
> "I know that whatever God does, it shall be forever. Nothing can be added to it, and nothing taken from it."

God's template is complete. It is perfect. It is eternally relevant. It is irreplaceable and cannot be redefined. There is no expiry date on it, and it will never become outdated. Nothing can be added to it. Nothing can be taken from it. You can't improve it by adding to it. Hell itself cannot alter it.

**God will do it, and man will glorify Him.**

---

## POINT 3 — ARROWS IN THE HAND OF A WARRIOR

> **Psalm 127:3-5**
> "Behold, children are a heritage from the Lord, the fruit of the womb is a reward. Like arrows in the hand of a warrior, so are the children of one's youth. Happy is the man who has his quiver full of them; they shall not be ashamed, but shall speak with their enemies in the gate."

Children are a blessing of the Lord — Paul called Timothy his son. Disciples, too, are a blessing of God. Like an arrow in the hand of a warrior, so are the disciples in the quiver of a leader who builds his life on God's template.

The end result of God's template is not shame, but courage, fearlessness, boldness, and victory. Those who follow it will not be timid. They will not be fearful. There will be no shame anywhere near their lives.

---

## POINT 4 — THE TEMPLATE PROVEN IN BATTLE

> **1 Chronicles 5:18**
> "The sons of Reuben, the Gadites, and half the tribe of Manasseh, valiant men, men able to bear shield and sword, and to shoot with the bow, and skillful in war, were forty-four thousand seven hundred and sixty, who went to war."

That is a huge army — but it was not Reuben, Gad, and Manasseh who built it. It was the result of their sons — their arrows — what had been invested in them, generation after generation. And the word tells us the outcome:

> **1 Chronicles 5:20, 22**
> "They cried out to God in the battle, and He heeded their entreaty, because they put their trust in Him... For many fell dead, because the war was God's."

They were not ashamed, because God was with them — and God was with them because they stuck to His template. You may be fighting battles right now, in your marriage, your workplace, your community, or for the soul of a nation. But there is a template, and when we return to it, we will not stand in shame.

These valiant men could bear three things — a shield, a sword, and a bow. Each one carries a meaning:

- **The shield** speaks of faith. For many, faith stays a mystery — talked about, but never worked. They don't know where to get it, how to grow it, how to protect it, or how to release it. They sit in church for decades without substance, because they've never been trained.
- **The sword** speaks of the word of God.

  > **Hebrews 4:12**
  > "For the word of God is living and powerful, and sharper than any two-edged sword, piercing even to the division of soul and spirit, and of joints and marrow, and is a discerner of the thoughts and intents of the heart."

  Some people have never been trained in the word. It stays a mystery — a cliché, just words. Lives are not changed by attending church; lives are transformed in the presence of God. Without that training, worship stays diluted, sacrifice never becomes a true crushing of the flesh.

- **The bow** speaks of discipleship — the instrument that sends the arrow twice as fast and twice as far as it could ever travel on its own. That is the privilege of discipleship: becoming the arrow in your leader's quiver. It speaks of relationship, of imitation.

  Jesus said He never did anything unless He saw the Father do it. That is why He did not end in shame — He ended in glory.

---

## POINT 5 — BECOME AN ARROW IN THE QUIVER

There are battles you will never win until you step into God's plan for discipleship — in your studies, in your marriage, in every area of life. Iron sharpens iron. When you place yourself in a quiver, you gain accountability, responsibility, and fruitfulness.

Every believer is an arrow. The question is: **who is propelling you?** Are you being shot toward shame, or is God sending you into a future of glory, victory, and conquest?

- **One arrow in a quiver is more powerful than a thousand arrows lying on the ground.**
- **One disciple is more powerful than a thousand church members.**

An archer without arrows can see the target but cannot reach it. And arrows left outside the quiver are exposed and unprotected. They don't last. They get stepped on, broken, forgotten, left behind. Their potential is wasted.

You can attend a cell group without ever becoming an arrow. Association is not the same as formation. Don't remain loose, lying on the ground. Get into a quiver. Get into a cell group. Allow yourself to be discipled — intentionally taught, corrected, shaped, and prepared, so that you can become like Christ, and become someone with arrows in your own quiver.

---

## CONCLUSION & DECLARATION

The template has already been given. It is our responsibility to find ourselves within it.

> **Matthew 28:19-20**
> "Go therefore, make disciples of all nations. Baptize them in the name of the Father, Son, and Holy Spirit. Teach them to obey my commands."

Come and declare this together:

> *"I repent of being a loose, lying, pointless, visionless, faithless arrow. I declare that I am part of a new breed — a generation of arrows that will not be wasted, that will not be bent or broken. I will position myself in a quiver, to be discipled in a relationship where I am accountable and intentional. I will contribute to bringing other arrows into the quiver, into relationship, into accountability, into discipleship. I will be trained to carry the shield of faith and the sword of the spirit. I will become skillful with the arrow and the bow. What was once a mystery will become my substance. I will not only be discipled — I will become a disciple maker, raising and preparing others for their assignment. My life will go faster, further, because I am an arrow in the hand of a warrior. My quiver will be filled with arrows that are prepared, sharpened, ready to be released at the right time. And I declare I will hit the target that was set before me. I will fulfill my assignment and live a godly legacy for future generations, in Jesus' name. Amen."*

There is no other template. There are some battles you will only overcome when you step into it. **I will not end in shame.**

---

## SALVATION CALL

You can only enter into this life of victory, into the kingdom of God, by becoming a child of God. You don't become a child of God by being a good person, or by being a churchgoer — you become a child of God by being *born* of God.

> **John 1:12**
> "But as many as received Him, to them He gave the right to become children of God, to those who believe in His name."

It doesn't matter if you grew up in a Christian home. As an individual, there must be a moment where you make the decision — to put your trust in the Lord.

If you have never made that decision, or if you once knew the Lord but have drifted and need to come back into the quiver, this is the moment. As long as it is called "today," don't harden your heart — be reconciled to God. There's urgency in that: some who left home this morning will not return there again.

*"My heavenly Father, I come to You in Jesus' name. Today I repent. Today I turn to You, the way that You want me to. Today I receive Jesus Christ as my Lord, as my template. Lord Jesus, please come into my life. Take out this old heart — give me a new heart. Wash me, cleanse me. And I thank You, Lord, that as I put my trust in You, I have the right to be called a child of God. I surrender my life to Your will, Your plan for my life. Come and take Your place in my life, in Jesus' name. Amen."*

Every curse is broken over your life. Today is a new beginning. The old is gone. Those who were far away have been brought near. Those who were in darkness are now in His marvelous light. Those who were strangers are now in His household.
